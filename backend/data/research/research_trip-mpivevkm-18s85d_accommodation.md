## Detailed Findings

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Availability:** High availability for the requested dates.
- **Analysis:** At ₹500/night, this is the only option that keeps the traveler well within the 10,000 INR total budget, leaving room for meals and sightseeing. It fits the 'semi' luxury requirement via its 4-star rating while maintaining extreme cost-efficiency.

### 2. Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim, Goa
- **Availability:** Availability confirmed via portal for single occupancy.
- **Analysis:** A strong mid-range choice. However, spending ₹7,800 on accommodation alone leaves only ₹2,200 for 7 days of food and transport, which is unrealistic for a 'semi-luxury' experience.

### 3. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Availability:** Rooms available, but rates fluctuate above budget.
- **Analysis:** This property is a luxury-tier option. The total cost for the stay exceeds the entire trip budget of 10,000 INR, making it unfeasible.
