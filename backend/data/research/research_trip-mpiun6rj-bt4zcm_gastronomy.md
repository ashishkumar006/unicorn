# 🌴 Gastronomy Research Report: Goa & Regional Variants

## 🍽️ Culinary Overview
While the dataset contains restaurants named 'Goa Restaurant', only one is physically located in the state of Goa (Mapusa). The others represent regional interpretations of Goan cuisine in New Delhi and Madhya Pradesh. This report focuses on the authentic Goan experience centered around the Mapusa region.

### 🍲 Local Specialties
Goan cuisine is a unique blend of Hindu and Portuguese influences. Key dishes to look for include:
- **Fish Curry Rice**: The quintessential Goan meal.
- **Vindaloo**: A spicy, vinegary pork dish.
- **Xacuti**: A complex coconut-based curry.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional Goan dessert.

### 📍 Dining Hubs & Food Alleys
- **Mapusa Market Area**: The heart of North Goa for authentic street eats and local spices.
- **Panjim Latin Quarter (Fontainhas)**: Ideal for heritage cafes and Portuguese-inspired sweets.

### 💰 Cost Analysis
- **Budget Range**: ₹500 - ₹900 per person.
- **Average Spend**: Approximately ₹660 across the analyzed spots.

### 📅 Booking Recommendations
- **Spice Goa Restaurant**: Casual dining; walk-ins are generally welcome, but early arrival is recommended during peak tourist seasons.
- **Urban Outposts (Delhi/MP)**: Since these are located in high-traffic zones like Dilli Haat, expect queues; no formal booking is usually required.
