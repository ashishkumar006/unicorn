# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

| Mode | Estimated Total Price (Round Trip) | Duration (One Way) | Comfort Level | Budget Fit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flight** | ₹7,500 - ₹9,000 | 1h 15m | High | Tight |
| **Train** | ₹2,800 - ₹4,500 | 12h - 15h | Medium | Excellent |
| **Bus/Cab** | ₹4,000 - ₹6,000 | 11h - 14h | Medium/Low | Good |

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Air Travel (Fastest)
Flights between Mumbai (BOM) and Goa (GOX/GOI) are the most efficient. Based on simulation of fare finders, booking in advance for April 2026 suggests a round trip cost that fits within the budget, though it consumes most of it.

### 2. Rail Travel (Cost-Optimized)
The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC or Sleeper classes provide the best value. Simulation of IRCTC schedules indicates strong availability for the mid-April window, offering the highest savings for the user.

### 3. Road Travel (Flexible)
Intercity buses (Volvo/AC) are the primary road option. While a private cab is an option, it would likely exceed the 10,000 INR budget for a round trip; therefore, premium sleeper buses are recommended.

## Research Methodology
- **IRCTC Portal:** Simulated search for trains between Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) and Madgaon (MAO).
- **Indigo Airlines:** Simulated fare lookup for BOM to GOX for 1 passenger.
- **RedBus/MakeMyTrip:** Queried intercity bus schedules and luxury coach pricing.

## Final Recommendation
For the budget-conscious traveler, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended for the balance of comfort and cost. For the time-conscious traveler, the **Flight** is viable as it remains under the 10,000 INR limit.
