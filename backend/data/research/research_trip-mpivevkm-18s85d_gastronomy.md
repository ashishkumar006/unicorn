## 🥥 Regional Culinary Specialties
To maximize the 10,000 INR budget, it is recommended to balance sit-down meals with local shacks and street food.

- **Fish Thali**: A staple comprising rice, fish curry, fried fish, and vegetable sides.
- **Bebinca**: The famous layered Goan cake.
- **Xacuti**: A spicy, coconut-based curry typically made with chicken or lamb.
