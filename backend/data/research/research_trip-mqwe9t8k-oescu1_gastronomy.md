## Detailed Analysis: Spice Goa Restaurant
Spice Goa offers a balanced mix of traditional Goan flavors and standard Indian fare. Given the ₹10,000 total trip budget, a ₹500 average cost per plate allows for approximately 10-15 high-quality meals, leaving ample room for street food and drinks.

## Local Culinary Recommendations
To complement the restaurant visit, travelers should seek out:
- **Fish Thalis**: Essential Goan experience found in local 'homes' and shacks.
- **Bebinca**: The legendary layered Goan cake.
- **Ros Omelette**: A staple street food breakfast found across Panjim and Mapusa.
