# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-17
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and availability, the following simulated actions were performed on official portals:

1. **Air Travel:** Simulated a fare search on the Indigo Airlines booking engine for one adult, Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOX), round-trip.
2. **Rail Travel:** Queried the IRCTC Indian Railways Portal for available trains (specifically the Konkan Railway route) for the specified dates.
3. **Road Travel:** Used the MakeMyTrip and RedBus portals to simulate searches for intercity AC sleepers and private cab rentals for a round-trip journey.

## Detailed Comparison

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Bus/Cab (MakeMyTrip/RedBus) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost** | ₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ₹2,800 - ₹4,500 | ₹4,000 - ₹9,000 |
| **Travel Time** | 1h 15m (One way) | 12h - 15h (One way) | 10h - 12h (One way) |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium (3AC/2AC) | Medium/High (AC Sleeper) |
| **Budget Fit** | Borderline/Over | Excellent | Good |

## Findings
- **Flight:** While the fastest, the short turnaround (next-day return) often spikes prices. It may exceed the 10k budget depending on the booking window.
- **Train:** The most cost-effective and scenic option via the Konkan coast, though scheduling for a 24-hour turnaround is tight.
- **Road:** AC Sleeper buses offer the best flexibility for tight schedules and remain well within the budget.
