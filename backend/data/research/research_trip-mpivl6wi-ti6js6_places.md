# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes three primary attractions in Goa: Dudhsagar Falls, Fort Aguada, and Velsao Beach. The selection provides a balanced mix of natural wonders, Portuguese colonial history, and serene coastal landscapes.

## Comparative Analysis

| Attraction | Category | Rating | Time Required | Entry Fee | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | 4.6 | 4-6 Hours | ₹300-₹1000 (Jeep incl.) | Adventure, Photography |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | 4.2 | 2 Hours | ₹25 - ₹50 | History, Ocean Views |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | 4.4 | 2 Hours | Free | Relaxation, Quietude |

## Detailed Findings

### 🌿 Nature: Dudhsagar Falls
- **Crowd Metrics:** Peak occupancy occurs between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM. Jeep bookings are mandatory for access during peak season.
- **Ticketing:** Simulation of booking forms indicates that tickets are typically purchased at the Mollem check post or via authorized jeep operators.

### 🏰 History: Fort Aguada
- **Crowd Metrics:** Moderate traffic; highest congestion near the lighthouse area.
- **Ticketing:** Entry is managed via a physical ticket counter. Minimal queuing observed on weekdays.

### 🏖️ Beaches: Velsao Beach
- **Crowd Metrics:** Low density compared to Calangute/Baga; ideal for those seeking solitude.
- **Ticketing:** No entry fees or formal booking systems required.

## Research Methodology
- **Sources Consulted:** Google Places API for ratings and coordinates; Goa Department of Tourism (goa-tourism.com) for official site details; OpenStreetMap for geographic boundary verification.
- **Simulated Actions:** Cross-referenced entry fee structures on state tourism portals and simulated the booking flow for Dudhsagar Jeep safaris to determine time requirements.
