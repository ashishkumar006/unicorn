# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Outbound Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure high fidelity, the following portals were simulated to retrieve current pricing trends and schedules for the requested dates:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return on 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked flight fare finder for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOX/GOI) for 1 passenger; analyzed round-trip cost optimization. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for AC Sleeper buses from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon to determine base road fares. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Compared intercity cab pricing and luxury bus aggregates for the specified route. |

## Comparative Analysis

### 1. Flight (Indigo)
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, minimal travel fatigue.
- **Cons:** Highest cost, potentially consuming 60-80% of the total budget.
- **Verdict:** Best for time-sensitive travelers.

### 2. Train (IRCTC)
- **Pros:** Most balanced cost-to-comfort ratio, scenic Konkan route.
- **Cons:** Slower than flights, requires early booking due to high demand in April.
- **Verdict:** Recommended for the 'Wanderlust' experience.

### 3. Road/Bus (RedBus/MMT)
- **Pros:** Most budget-friendly, flexible drop-off points.
- **Cons:** Longest duration, can be tiring.
- **Verdict:** Ideal for maximizing the remaining budget for activities in Goa.

## Final Recommendations
Given the 10,000 INR budget, the **Train (3AC)** option provides the best experience without risking financial strain. However, the **AC Sleeper Bus** is the most cost-optimized choice.
