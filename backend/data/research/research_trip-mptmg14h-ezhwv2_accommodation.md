# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip

## Overview
**Traveler(s):** 1
**Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (7 Nights)
**Budget:** 10,000 INR (Total)
**Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Hotel Comparison Analysis

| Hotel | Rating | Stars | Estimated Total (7 Nights) | Budget Alignment | Recommendation |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Shelsta Holiday Resort** | 4.3 | 4 | ~3,500 INR | Under Budget | **Highly Recommended** (Best for budget) |
| **Hard Rock Hotel Goa** | 4.6 | 5 | ~9,100 INR | At Budget Limit | **Recommended** (Semi-Luxury feel) |
| **The Fern Kadamba Hotel** | 4.3 | 4 | ~14,700 INR | Over Budget | **Not Recommended** (Exceeds 10k limit) |

### Detailed Findings

#### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Analysis:** This is the most economical choice, leaving significant room in the budget for activities and dining. It offers a strong balance of quality (4 stars) and affordability.
- **Pros:** Extremely budget-friendly, high guest rating.
- **Cons:** Less "luxury" appeal compared to Hard Rock.

#### 2. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Analysis:** This fits the "Semi-Luxury" requirement perfectly. While it consumes nearly the entire 10,000 INR budget, it provides a 5-star experience. 
- **Pros:** Brand prestige, high-end amenities, music-themed atmosphere.
- **Cons:** Leaves very little budget for other expenses.

#### 3. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Analysis:** While a quality Marriott-affiliated property, the projected cost for 7 nights exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR.
- **Pros:** Professional service, spa facilities.
- **Cons:** Cost-prohibitive for this specific budget constraint.
