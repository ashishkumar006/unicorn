# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and scheduling, the following simulated actions were performed on official booking portals:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Queried flight fare finder for 1 adult from BOM to GOI for April 16 and return April 22. |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Searched rail schedules and fare availability for Mumbai CSMT/LTT to Madgaon (MAO) for specified dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for AC Sleeper bus options from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon for 1 passenger. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Checked intercity cab estimates and combined transport bundles. |

## Comparison and Findings

### 1. Flight (The Premium Choice)
While the fastest, round-trip flights on Indigo closely approach the total budget limit. Prices for April are typically higher due to the transition into the peak shoulder season. 

### 2. Train (The Balanced Choice)
The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. Using a 3AC or 2AC berth provides a comfortable balance between cost and convenience, fitting well within the 10,000 INR budget.

### 3. Road/Bus (The Budget Choice)
Intercity AC Sleeper buses are the most cost-effective. This option leaves significant room in the budget for local transport within Goa.

## Final Recommendation
For a balance of cost-optimization and comfort, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended. For maximum time efficiency, the **Flight** is viable but leaves little room for other expenses.
