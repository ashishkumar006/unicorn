# Goa Tourism Comprehensive Guide

## 🏖️ Beaches & Coastal
- **Calangute & Baga**: The hub of North Goa's nightlife and water sports.
- **Palolem**: A serene, crescent-shaped beach in the South, ideal for kayaking.
- **Colva**: Famous for its white sands and long shoreline.

## 🏛️ History & Heritage
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus**: A UNESCO World Heritage site containing the remains of St. Francis Xavier.
- **Fort Aguada**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort with a lighthouse overlooking the Arabian Sea.
- **Old Goa**: The center of Portuguese colonial architecture.

## 🌿 Nature & Wellness
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A majestic four-tiered waterfall cascading down the Western Ghats.
- **Spice Plantations**: Immersive tours into the cultivation of tropical spices.

### 💡 Travel Tips
- **Best Time to Visit**: November to February (Pleasant weather).
- **Transport**: Renting a scooter or car is the most efficient way to explore.
- **Dress Code**: Modest clothing is required for entering churches in Old Goa.
