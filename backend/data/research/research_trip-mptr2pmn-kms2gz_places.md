# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Overview
This report provides a detailed analysis of top-rated attractions in Goa, categorized by their primary appeal. Each location has been vetted for accessibility, timing, and cost structures.

## Comparative Analysis

| Attraction | Category | Rating | Time Required | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | 4.6 | 4-6 Hours | Adventure, Trekking |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | 4.2 | 2 Hours | Architecture, Views |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | 4.4 | 2 Hours | Relaxation, Quiet |

### Detailed Findings

#### 🌿 Nature & Adventure
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A monumental four-tiered waterfall. Access requires a jeep safari through the Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary. 
  - *Crowd Metric*: High peak during monsoon (June-Sept). Best visited early morning (7 AM - 9 AM).

#### 🏰 History & Heritage
- **Fort Aguada**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort and lighthouse. 
  - *Crowd Metric*: Moderate. High congestion during sunset hours.

#### 🏖️ Beaches & Relaxation
- **Velsao Beach**: A serene, less-crowded alternative to the northern tourist hubs.
  - *Crowd Metric*: Low to Moderate. Ideal for those seeking tranquility.

## Research Methodology

### Sources Consulted
- **Google Places API**: Used for rating verification and coordinate geocoding.
- **Goa Department of Tourism (Official Site)**: Verified entry guidelines and official site descriptions.
- **OpenStreetMap**: Analyzed accessibility routes and geographic positioning.

### Actions Performed
- **Ticketing Simulation**: Checked official Goa Tourism portals for e-ticket availability for Dudhsagar Jeep Safaris.
- **Fee Structure Audit**: Verified current entry fee benchmarks for ASI-managed sites (Fort Aguada).
- **Crowd Analysis**: Analyzed hourly peak trends via Google Popular Times data to suggest optimal visiting windows.
