# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To determine the most cost-optimized and efficient routes, the following simulations were performed on official booking portals:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried train schedules for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for April 16 and return April 22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Simulated fare search for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOX/GOI) for 1 passenger. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Checked AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper bus availability and fares for Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Compared intercity cab fares and luxury bus options. |

## Comparison and Findings

### 1. Flight (Indigo)
- **Analysis:** The fastest option. While base fares are competitive, the total round-trip cost often pushes the budget limit once taxes are added. However, for a single traveler, it remains the most convenient.
- **Verdict:** Recommended for those prioritizing time over cost.

### 2. Train (IRCTC)
- **Analysis:** The most cost-effective and scenic option (Konkan Railway). The 3AC class provides a balance of comfort and price, fitting well within the 10,000 INR budget.
- **Verdict:** Best value for money.

### 3. Road/Bus (RedBus)
- **Analysis:** Highly flexible with multiple departure timings. AC Sleeper buses are reliable, though road travel can be tiring due to the duration.
- **Verdict:** Recommended as a flexible alternative to rail.
