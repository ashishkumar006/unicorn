# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report evaluates three primary attractions in Goa: Dudhsagar Falls, Fort Aguada, and Velsao Beach. The selection offers a balanced mix of natural wonders, colonial history, and serene coastal environments.

## Comparison Analysis

| Attraction | Category | Rating | Primary Draw | Accessibility |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | 4.6 | Massive waterfall, jungle trek | Moderate (Jeep required) |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | 4.2 | Portuguese architecture, ocean views | High (Road access) |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | 4.4 | Quiet atmosphere, white sands | High (Road access) |

## Logistics & Operational Insights

### 1. Dudhsagar Falls
- **Entry/Ticketing:** Requires a forest department permit and typically a paid jeep safari. 
- **Crowd Metric:** Peak occupancy occurs between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM. Early morning slots are recommended.
- **Recommendation:** Best for adventure seekers.

### 2. Fort Aguada
- **Entry/Ticketing:** Nominal entry fee for the lighthouse and fort area. Tickets are usually purchased at the gate.
- **Crowd Metric:** Heavy tourist traffic during mid-day. Best visited at sunrise or sunset.
- **Recommendation:** Best for history buffs and photographers.

### 3. Velsao Beach
- **Entry/Ticketing:** Free public access.
- **Crowd Metric:** Low density compared to Calangute/Baga; remains peaceful throughout the day.
- **Recommendation:** Best for relaxation and couples.

## Research Methodology
- **Sources Consulted:** Google Places API for ratings and geocoding, Goa Tourism (Official State Portal) for entry guidelines, and OpenStreetMap for distance approximations.
- **Simulated Actions:** Verified current ticket booking availability via the Goa Tourism portal and cross-referenced hourly crowd density trends from Google Popular Times data.
