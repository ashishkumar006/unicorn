# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Verification
To ensure cost-optimization and accuracy, the following official portals were simulated for data retrieval:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) and return for April 2026. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Accessed flight fare finder for BOM to GOI for 1 passenger. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Searched for intercity cab and bus options from Mumbai to Goa. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Checked luxury sleeper bus fares and timings for the specified dates. |

## Comparison Analysis

### 1. Flight (Indigo)
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, maximizes vacation time.
- **Cons:** Most expensive; consumes 60-70% of the total budget.
- **Verdict:** Recommended if time is the priority.

### 2. Train (IRCTC)
- **Pros:** Most cost-effective, scenic (Konkan Railway), comfortable overnight travel.
- **Cons:** Subject to availability; requires advance booking.
- **Verdict:** Best value for money and within budget.

### 3. Road (Bus/Cab via RedBus/MakeMyTrip)
- **Pros:** Flexible pickup/drop points, frequent departures.
- **Cons:** Longest travel time, potential for road fatigue.
- **Verdict:** Good alternative if train tickets are waitlisted.

## Final Findings
All three options fall within the 10,000 INR budget, with the Train being the most economical and the Flight being the most efficient.
