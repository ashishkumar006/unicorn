## 🔍 Restaurant Analysis

### Spice Goa Restaurant (Mapusa, Goa)
- **Analysis:** A highly-rated local establishment specializing in regional Goan cuisine. With an average cost of ₹500 per person, it fits comfortably within the ₹10,000 budget, allowing for multiple high-quality meals.
- **Vibe:** Casual, family-friendly, and traditional.
- **Recommendation:** Ideal for experiencing authentic North Goan flavors without the 'tourist premium' found in Calangute or Baga.

## 🥘 Regional Culinary Specialties
To maximize the budget, it is recommended to mix restaurant dining with street-side delicacies:
1. **Fish Thali:** The cornerstone of Goan lunch; usually includes fish curry, fried fish, rice, and vegetables.
2. **Bebinca:** A multi-layered Goan pudding.
3. **Ros Omelette:** A street-side favorite consisting of an omelette topped with spicy gravy.
