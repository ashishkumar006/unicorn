# Transit Options: Mumbai to Goa

### 1. Flight (Fastest Option)
- **Airline:** IndiGo
- **Route:** BOM $\rightarrow$ GOX (Manohar International Airport)
- **Duration:** 1h 15m
- **Cost:** ₹4,500 - ₹6,000
- **Pros:** Extremely time-efficient, comfortable.

### 2. Train (Scenic/Budget Option)
- **Train:** Tejas Express / Konkan Railway
- **Route:** CSMT/Dadar $\rightarrow$ Madgaon (MAO)
- **Duration:** 11h 30m
- **Cost:** ₹1,200 - ₹2,800 (depending on class)
- **Pros:** Beautiful Western Ghats scenery, cost-effective.

### 3. Road/Cab (Flexible Option)
- **Service:** Private Intercity Cab / Shared Taxi
- **Route:** NH 66 / AH 47
- **Duration:** 10h - 12h
- **Cost:** ₹7,000 - ₹9,000 (One-way private) / ₹2,500 (Shared)
- **Pros:** Door-to-door service, flexible stops.

**Budget Status:** All options fall within the ₹10,000 limit.
