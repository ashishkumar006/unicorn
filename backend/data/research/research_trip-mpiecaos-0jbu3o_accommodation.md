# 🏨 Goa Accommodation Research Report

## 🌟 Executive Summary
Based on a budget of **10,000 INR** and a **semi-luxury** preference for 1 traveler, I have analyzed three distinct options in Goa. All selected hotels are 4-star properties, fitting the semi-luxury criteria while staying well within the overall budget limit.

## 🔍 Detailed Analysis

### 1. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa Goa
**The Premium Choice**
- **Analysis:** This is the best fit for the "Semi-Luxury" requirement. As a Marriott-affiliated property, it offers the highest standard of service and facilities, including a full-service spa.
- **Budget Impact:** At ₹2,100/night, a 4-night stay would cost ₹8,400, leaving ₹1,600 for extras.
- **Best for:** Travelers seeking wellness and a corporate-luxury feel.
- **[Official Website](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/goifk-the-fern-kadamba-hotel-and-spa-goa-panjim-series/overview/?scid=f2ae0541-1279-4f24-b197-a979c79310b0)**

### 2. Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim
**The Balanced Choice**
- **Analysis:** Located in the heart of Candolim, this hotel balances luxury with accessibility. It is ideal for those who want to be near the beach and nightlife without sacrificing hotel quality.
- **Budget Impact:** Very economical at ₹1,300/night, allowing for a longer stay or higher spending on dining.
- **Best for:** Social travelers and explorers.
- **[Official Website](https://www.lemontreehotels.com/lemon-tree-hotel/goa/candolim-goa?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gmb_listing)**

### 3. Shelsta Holiday Resort
**The Budget-Luxury Choice**
- **Analysis:** Surprisingly priced at ₹500/night while maintaining a 4-star rating. While it fits the budget perfectly, it is more of a 'value-luxury' option than 'semi-luxury'.
- **Budget Impact:** Minimal; leaves significant room in the 10k budget.
- **Best for:** Extreme budget conscious travelers who still want 4-star amenities.
- **[Official Website](http://www.shelstaholidayresort.com/)**

## 🛠️ Booking Guidance
- **Occupancy:** All listed options typically allow single occupancy for the quoted rates. Ensure you select "1 Adult" during the booking process to maintain these prices.
- **Recommendation:** I highly recommend **The Fern Kadamba** to maximize the "semi-luxury" experience, as it aligns most closely with the desired style while remaining well under the 10,000 INR cap.
