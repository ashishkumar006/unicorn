# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Overview
This research report provides a detailed comparison of key tourist landmarks in Goa, focusing on accessibility, entry requirements, and visitor experience. 

## Comparative Analysis

| Attraction | Category | Rating | Entry Fee | Duration | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | 4.6 | Variable (Jeep/Forest) | 4-6 hours | Adventure, Photography |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | 4.2 | Low | 1-2 hours | History, Ocean Views |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | 4.4 | Free | 2 hours | Relaxation, Quietude |

## Ticketing & Occupancy Insights
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: Requires forest department permits. Peak occupancy is seen between 9 AM and 12 PM. Jeep safari bookings are highly recommended in advance.
- **Fort Aguada**: Operates on a walk-in basis. Crowd metrics indicate peak congestion during sunset hours (5 PM - 7 PM).
- **Velsao Beach**: Public access beach; no ticketing required. Lowest crowd density compared to North Goa beaches.

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure data accuracy, the following steps were performed:
1. **Data Retrieval**: Queried Google Places API for current ratings and coordinates.
2. **Official Verification**: Accessed the Goa State Tourism Board portals to verify entry fee structures and official booking links.
3. **Mapping**: Used OpenStreetMap to validate the geospatial distribution of the selected spots.
4. **Simulation**: Simulated ticket booking flows on local tourism sites to determine the availability of online pre-booking versus on-site payment.
