# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Dining Exploration

## Executive Summary
Based on the budget of 10,000 INR, the focus is on authentic Goan flavors and high-value dining. While the provided dataset contained some outliers (restaurants located outside of Goa), we have filtered for the authentic Goan experience focusing on **Spice Goa Restaurant** and traditional regional specialties.

## Restaurant Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant
- **Verdict**: High Value / Authentic
- **Analysis**: This establishment offers a balanced price-to-quality ratio. With an average cost of ₹500 per person, it fits perfectly within a 10k budget for a multi-day trip, allowing for luxury additions elsewhere.
- **Vibe**: Casual family dining with a focus on regional spices.

*Note: Other listed 'Goa Restaurants' in Delhi and MP were excluded from recommendations as they are not located within the destination geography.*

## Local Culinary Specialties
To maximize the 10,000 INR budget, we recommend integrating local markets and shacks:
- **Goan Fish Curry**: A staple available at Spice Goa and beach shacks.
- **Bebinca**: The traditional layered cake, best found in Mapusa markets.
- **Ros Omelette**: A street food essential for breakfast.

## Budget Allocation Strategy
- **Mid-range Dining (Spice Goa)**: ₹2,000 (2-3 meals)
- **Street Food & Markets**: ₹3,000 (Daily snacks/breakfast)
- **Beach Shack Dinners**: ₹5,000 (Fresh seafood)
