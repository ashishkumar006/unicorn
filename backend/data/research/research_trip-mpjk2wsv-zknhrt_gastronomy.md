# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Culinary Exploration

## Executive Summary
Based on the analyzed data, the primary focus for a budget of 10,000 INR is concentrated on authentic Goan flavors. Note: Initial data filtering removed options located outside the state of Goa (Delhi and MP) to ensure geographical accuracy for the traveler.

## Local Culinary Recommendations
To maximize the budget, it is recommended to balance seated restaurant meals with local 'shacks' and street markets. Focus on seafood-based curries and traditional baked goods.
