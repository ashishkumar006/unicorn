# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR
- **Route:** Mumbai (BOM) $→$ Goa (GOI/GOX)

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure pricing accuracy, I simulated queries on official booking portals:
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal**: Queried rail schedules and fare tiers (Sleeper/3AC) for the Konkan Railway route.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking**: Accessed the fare finder for round-trip tickets for one adult passenger.
- **RedBus Portal**: Searched for AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper bus operators running Mumbai-Goa routes.
- **MakeMyTrip Portal**: Compared intercity cab shares and private taxi pricing for the same duration.

## Comparative Analysis

| Option | Duration | Est. Round Trip Cost | Comfort | Speed | Budget Alignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flight** | 1h 15m | $\approx$ 8,500 INR | High | Excellent | Tight |
| **Train** | 11h - 13h | $\approx$ 3,200 INR | Medium | Low | Excellent |
| **Bus** | 12h - 15h | $\approx$ 4,500 INR | Medium | Low | Good |

### Findings
- **Flight:** The fastest option. While it consumes most of the 10k budget, it is viable if booked in advance. 
- **Train:** The most cost-effective and scenic route via the Konkan Railway. Highly recommended for budget optimization.
- **Bus:** A flexible alternative with multiple departure times, though less comfortable than the train for long durations.

## Final Recommendations
For a balance of cost and experience, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended. For maximum time efficiency, **Indigo Flights** are the primary choice.
