# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1 Adult
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (6 nights)
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR (Total)
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Detailed Hotel Analysis

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Estimated Total:** ~3,000 INR
- **Verdict:** **Best Budget Fit.** This is the only option that comfortably fits within the total trip budget of 10,000 INR while leaving room for food and activities.
- **Findings:** High value for money, though it leans more towards 'Budget' than 'Semi-Luxury'.

### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa Goa
- **Estimated Total:** ~7,800 INR
- **Verdict:** **Budget Tight.** While the room rate fits, it consumes nearly 80% of the total trip budget, leaving very little for other expenses.
- **Findings:** High-quality service and amenities, fitting the 'Semi-Luxury' profile perfectly.

### 3. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Estimated Total:** ~12,600 INR
- **Verdict:** **Exceeds Budget.** The cost for accommodation alone exceeds the entire trip budget of 10,000 INR.
- **Findings:** Luxury experience, but financially non-viable for this specific budget constraint.

## Research Methodology

### Sources Checked
- Google Places API (for base ratings and coordinates)
- Official Hotel Portals (Shelsta, Marriott/Fern, Hard Rock)
