# Tourist Attractions Research Report: Goa

## Overview
This report provides a detailed analysis of key landmarks in Goa, focusing on accessibility, timing, and entry requirements to optimize visitor scheduling.

## Comparative Analysis

| Attraction | Category | Avg. Rating | Time Required | Entry Fee | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | 4.6 | 4-6 hours | Variable (Jeep/Entry) | Adventure, Nature |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | 4.2 | 2 hours | Nominal | Architecture, Views |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | 4.4 | 2-3 hours | Free | Relaxation, Quietude |

## Operational Insights
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: Requires significant travel time and a jeep safari. Peak crowds occur between 10 AM and 2 PM.
- **Fort Aguada**: High footfall during sunset hours. Tickets are generally handled at the entrance gate.
- **Velsao Beach**: A low-density alternative to North Goa beaches, ideal for those avoiding crowds.

## Research Methodology & Verification
### Sources Consulted
- **Google Places API**: Used for rating aggregation and geocoding verification.
- **Goa Tourism Board (Official Site)**: Used for verifying historical facts and operational status.
- **OpenStreetMap (OSM)**: Used to calculate accessibility and distance from main transit hubs.

### Actions Performed
- **Simulated Ticket Booking**: Accessed the Goa Tourism portal to check for online pre-booking availability for Dudhsagar and Fort Aguada.
- **Entry Fee Audit**: Cross-referenced local tourism board fee structures for monument entries.
- **Crowd Metric Analysis**: Simulated hourly occupancy trends based on historical visitor data to determine "Best Visit Times."
