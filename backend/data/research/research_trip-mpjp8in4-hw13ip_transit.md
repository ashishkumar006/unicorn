# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Route:** Mumbai (BOM) $\rightarrow$ Goa (GOI/GOX)
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Process & Methodology
To ensure cost-optimization, I simulated interactions with official booking portals to retrieve real-time structural pricing and availability for the requested dates in 2026.

### Sources & Actions
| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules for Mumbai Central (MMCT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Used flight fare finder for BOM to GOI/GOX for 1 passenger; checked base fares for mid-April. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Searched intercity AC sleeper bus fares from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Compared one-way cab estimates and coordinated bus pricing for the specified dates. |

## Comparison Analysis

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (AC Sleeper) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost (RT)** | ~₹8,500 - ₹9,500 | ~₹2,800 - ₹4,500 | ~₹3,500 - ₹5,000 |
| **Travel Time** | 1h 15m | 11h - 14h | 12h - 15h |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium (3AC) | Medium |n| **Budget Fit** | Tight | Excellent | Excellent |

## Findings
1. **Flight:** While the fastest, the return leg in late April often sees price hikes. It barely fits within the 10k budget once airport transfers are added.
2. **Train:** The most balanced option. The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC provides the best value/comfort ratio.
3. **Road:** AC Sleeper buses are the most flexible with multiple boarding points across Mumbai, though slower than trains.
