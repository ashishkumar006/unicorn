# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1 Adult
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (7 Nights)
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR (Total)
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Executive Summary
After conducting a deep rate analysis and availability simulation for the selected properties, there is a significant discrepancy between the provided estimated prices and the actual market rates for a 7-day stay. The total budget of 10,000 INR allows for a maximum nightly rate of approximately **1,428 INR**. Only one property aligns closely with this budget, while the luxury options far exceed the total trip budget.

## Research Findings
1. **Budget Constraints:** The user's total budget of 10,000 INR for 7 days is extremely tight for "Semi-Luxury" 4-5 star hotels in Goa during April. Most professional hotels in these categories start at 4,000 INR/night.
2. **Availability:** Simulated checks indicate availability for April 2026; however, the rates provided in the initial seed data (e.g., 500 INR/night) appear to be placeholders and do not reflect actual market pricing for these branded hotels.
3. **Value Proposition:** Lemon Tree Candolim offers the best proximity to beaches for a single traveler, though it requires a budget increase.
