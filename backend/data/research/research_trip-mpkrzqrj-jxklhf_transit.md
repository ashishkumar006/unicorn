# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## 1. Executive Summary
Travel from Mumbai to Goa for one passenger (2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22). The primary goal is cost optimization within a 10,000 INR budget. All options analyzed fall within this budget.

## 2. Transit Options Analysis

### Option 1: Air Travel (Fastest)
- **Carrier:** IndiGo
- **Route:** BOM $\rightarrow$ GOX (Manohar International Airport)
- **Pros:** Extremely time-efficient, avoids long road transit.
- **Cons:** Higher cost relative to rail/bus, requires airport commutes.

### Option 2: Rail Travel (Balanced)
- **Carrier:** IRCTC (Konkan Railway)
- **Route:** CSMT/LTT $\rightarrow$ Madgaon (MAO)
- **Pros:** Highly scenic (Western Ghats), cost-effective, comfortable sleepers.
- **Cons:** Longer duration, subject to availability/waitlists.

### Option 3: Road/Bus (Budget)
- **Carrier:** Private AC Sleeper via RedBus/MMT
- **Route:** Mumbai Central $\rightarrow$ Panjim/Mapusa
- **Pros:** Lowest cost, flexible drop-off points.
- **Cons:** Most fatiguing, traffic dependent.

## 4. Research Methodology
### Sources Consulted
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal**: Checked for Konkan Railway and Tejas Express schedules.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking**: Simulated fare search for BOM-GOX route.
- **RedBus Portal / MakeMyTrip**: Queried intercity sleeper bus availability.

### Actions Performed
- Simulated passenger count (1) and date selection (2026-04-16 / 2026-04-22) on Indigo's booking engine.
- Queried the IRCTC rail schedules search form for Mumbai to Madgaon (MAO).
- Filled out the RedBus search form for Mumbai to Goa AC Sleeper routes to verify pricing trends.
