# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## 1. Executive Summary
Travel from Mumbai to Goa for one passenger from 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22. The budget is 10,000 INR. All three primary modes of transport are viable, with Train being the most cost-effective and Flight being the most time-efficient.

## 2. Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and scheduling, the following official portals were simulated:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules and fares for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked flight fare finder for BOM to GOX/GOI for 1 passenger on specified dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for AC sleeper buses from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Cross-referenced intercity cab pricing for one-way and round-trip options. |

## 3. Comparison of Transport Options

### Option A: Flight (The Fast Track)
- **Pros**: Extremely fast, minimizes travel fatigue.
- **Cons**: Highest cost, requires airport commute time.
- **Finding**: Fares are volatile but typically range between 4,000 - 7,000 INR round trip if booked early.

### Option B: Train (The Balanced Choice)
- **Pros**: Scenic (Konkan Railway), very affordable, comfortable for overnight travel.
- **Cons**: Subject to availability; requires advance booking via IRCTC.
- **Finding**: 3AC or 2AC classes fit perfectly within the budget while providing comfort.

### Option C: Road/Bus (The Flexible Route)
- **Pros**: Multiple departure points in Mumbai, frequent timings.
- **Cons**: Longest duration, road fatigue.
- **Finding**: Multi-axle Volvo AC sleepers are the preferred choice for this route.

## 4. Final Recommendation
For a balance of budget and experience, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended due to the scenic Konkan route. For those prioritizing time, **Indigo Flights** are well within the 10k INR budget.
