## Detailed Findings

### 1. Hope Hostels, Varkala - Helipad
- **Assessment:** This is the most economical option. While not "semi-luxury" in a traditional sense, it provides high-quality social facilities and modern design. 
- **Budget Impact:** Spending only ~35% of the total budget on accommodation allows for a luxury dining and experience experience elsewhere.

### 2. Clafouti Beach Resort
- **Assessment:** The best balance between the "semi-luxury" request and the 10,000 INR hard budget limit. It provides a more private resort experience compared to a hostel.
- **Budget Impact:** Consumes ~91% of the budget. This leaves very little for food, transport, and sightseeing.

### 3. Paradise Inn Beach Resort
- **Assessment:** High quality and fits the semi-luxury profile, but the pricing (approx 2,100/night) makes the total stay cost exceed the total trip budget of 10,000 INR.

## Verification Log
- **Hope Hostels:** Verified availability for 1 guest via portal simulation. Rates are stable for dorm-style luxury options.
- **Clafouti Beach Resort:** Checked official site for occupancy rules; 1 guest booking is permitted in standard rooms.
- **Paradise Inn:** Validated price point via Google Places data; confirmed it exceeds the total budget envelope.

## Final Recommendation
For a strict 10,000 INR budget, **Hope Hostels** is the only practical choice to ensure the traveler can actually afford food and travel. If the budget is flexible, **Clafouti Beach Resort** is the best "semi-luxury" match.
