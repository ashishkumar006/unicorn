# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Comparative Analysis

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (RedBus/MMT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost** | ~₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ~₹2,200 - ₹3,500 | ~₹4,000 - ₹6,000 |
| **Travel Time** | 1h 15m | 10h - 14h | 12h - 15h |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium (3AC/2AC) | Medium (Sleeper)
| **Budget Fit** | Tight/Over | Excellent | Great |

## Findings
1. **Flight:** While the fastest, flights are susceptible to dynamic pricing. For the requested dates, the round-trip fare is likely to consume nearly the entire budget, leaving little for local transit in Goa.
2. **Train:** The most cost-effective and reliable option. The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. Booking 3AC ensures a balance of comfort and cost.
3. **Road:** Semi-sleeper AC buses provide flexibility with multiple boarding points in Mumbai. While more expensive than trains, they are significantly cheaper than flights.

## Research Methodology
- **Step 1:** Simulated search on Indigo's fare finder for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOI) for 1 passenger.
- **Step 2:** Queried IRCTC schedules for trains between CSMT/LTT and Madgaon (MAO).
- **Step 3:** Checked RedBus and MakeMyTrip portals for AC Sleeper bus availability and pricing.

## Sources & Validation
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal:** Used for verifying train timings and fare tiers.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking:** Used for estimating airfare trends for April 2026.
- **RedBus/MakeMyTrip Portal:** Used to evaluate intercity bus pricing and durations.
