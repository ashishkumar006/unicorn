# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Dining Analysis

Based on the provided data and regional analysis, the primary focus is on authentic Goan flavors. Note: The input data contained entries located in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh; these have been filtered out to ensure the itinerary remains strictly within Goa to optimize the 10,000 INR budget.

## Restaurant Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant (Mapusa)
- **Verdict**: Highly recommended for travelers seeking authentic regional flavors without breaking the bank.
- **Cost Analysis**: With an average plate cost of ₹500, this fits perfectly within a mid-range budget for a multi-day trip.
- **Atmosphere**: Local, welcoming, and authentic.

## Regional Culinary Insights
Goan cuisine is a fusion of Portuguese and Indian influences. For a budget of 10k, focusing on *Thalis* (platters) provides the best value and variety.
