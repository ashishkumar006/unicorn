# Accommodation Research Report: Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (6 Nights)
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR (Total)
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Executive Summary
Based on the budget of 10,000 INR for 6 nights, the average nightly budget is approximately **1,666 INR**. Only two of the three researched properties fit within this financial constraint. While 'The Fern Kadamba' offers the highest luxury level, it exceeds the total budget. 'Shelsta Holiday Resort' provides the best value, whereas 'Lemon Tree' offers a balanced mid-range experience.

## Hotel Analysis

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Findings:** Extremely budget-friendly. Ideal for travelers wanting to save their budget for activities/dining.
- **Pros:** High rating for price point, significantly under budget.
- **Cons:** More basic amenities compared to chain hotels.

### 2. Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim
- **Findings:** Fits the "Semi-Luxury" requirement perfectly. It is a branded experience that stays within the 10,000 INR limit.
- **Pros:** Professional service, reliable standards, great location in Candolim.
- **Cons:** Higher cost than Shelsta, leaving less room for other trip expenses.

### 3. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Findings:** Exceeds the total budget by 2,600 INR. While high quality, it is not financially viable for this specific constraint.
- **Pros:** Full spa services, Marriott standards.
- **Cons:** Price exceeds the total 10k budget.

## Research Methodology
- **Sources:** Google Places API and official hotel booking portals.
