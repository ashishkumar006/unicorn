# Goa Tourist Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This research evaluates three distinct attractions in Goa: **Dudhsagar Falls**, **Fort Aguada**, and **Velsao Beach**. The selection provides a balanced mix of natural wonders, colonial history, and serene coastal experiences.

## Detailed Analysis

### 🌿 Nature & Wildlife
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A majestic multi-tiered waterfall. Due to its remote location, it requires significant travel time. Peak crowds occur between October and March.

### 🏰 History & Architecture
- **Fort Aguada**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort and lighthouse. It offers panoramic views of the Arabian Sea. High pedestrian traffic is noted during late afternoons.

### 🏖️ Coastal Leisure
- **Velsao Beach**: A quiet alternative to the commercial hubs of North Goa. Ideal for low-density tourism and relaxation.
