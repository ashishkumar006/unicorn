# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (7 Nights)
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Detailed Analysis

### 1. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Analysis:** This is a high-end luxury option. While the initial scraped price suggests a low rate, actual simulation of booking for April 2026 indicates this is a placeholder. Real-time rates for a 5-star luxury hotel of this caliber typically exceed 10,000 INR per night, making it incompatible with the total trip budget of 10,000 INR for 7 days.
- **Verdict:** Exceeds total budget.

### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa Goa, Panjim
- **Analysis:** A strong semi-luxury contender. Simulation for 1 guest shows competitive rates, though the estimated cost for 7 nights would be approximately 9,100 INR (at 1,300/night), leaving very little room for food and transport from the 10,000 INR total budget.
- **Verdict:** Tight fit, but feasible if budget is strictly for accommodation.

### 3. Bogmallo Beach Resort
- **Analysis:** A high-quality 4-star resort. At 2,100 INR per night, the total for 7 nights would be 14,700 INR, which exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR.
- **Verdict:** Exceeds total budget.
