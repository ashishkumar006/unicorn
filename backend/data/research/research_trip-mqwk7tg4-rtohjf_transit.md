# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Route:** Mumbai (BOM) $\rightarrow$ Goa (GOI/GOX)
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and scheduling, the following official portals were simulated for data extraction:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules search form for Mumbai Central (MMCT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return on 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Accessed flight fare finder for BOM to GOI; simulated passenger count: 1. Checked return fares for 2026-04-22. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Queried intercity bus search form for Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon (Sleeper/Semi-Sleeper) for the specified dates. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Simulated one-way and round-trip cab fare estimation for Mumbai to Goa. |

## Comparison of Transport Options

### 1. Flight (Indigo Airlines)
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, time-efficient.
- **Cons:** Highest cost, proximity to budget limit.
- **Finding:** Round-trip fares are estimated at ~7,500 - 9,000 INR depending on booking lead time. Fits within the 10k budget but leaves little for local transit.

### 2. Train (Indian Railways - Konkan Railway)
- **Pros:** Most cost-effective, scenic route via Western Ghats.
- **Cons:** Longer duration, requires advance booking (Tatkal/General).
- **Finding:** 3AC/2AC fares are very affordable (~2,500 - 4,000 INR round trip), providing the best balance of comfort and cost.

### 3. Road/Bus (Intercity Sleeper)
- **Pros:** Flexible timings, direct drops to Panjim.
- **Cons:** Road fatigue, variable safety ratings.
- **Finding:** Private sleepers via RedBus range from 2,000 - 3,500 INR round trip.

## Final Recommendation
For a balance of budget optimization and experience, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended. It saves approximately 6,000 INR compared to the budget limit, which can be reallocated to accommodation and activities in Goa.
