# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR
- **Route:** Mumbai (BOM) $\leftrightarrow$ Goa (GOI/GOX)

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and scheduling, the following actions were performed:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Simulated fare finder for 1 passenger: BOM to GOI on 2026-04-16 and return on 2026-04-22. |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules search form for Konkan Railway route (Mumbai CST/LTT to Madgaon/Thivim) for specified dates. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Searched intercity cab and bus aggregates for Mumbai to Goa one-way and round-trip pricing. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for sleeper bus fares from Mumbai to Goa for April 16th and 22nd. |

## Comparison of Options

### 1. Flight (Indigo)
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, saves significant time for a short trip.
- **Cons:** Most expensive option; consumes ~60-70% of the total budget.

### 2. Train (IRCTC - Konkan Railway)
- **Pros:** Highly scenic (Western Ghats), budget-friendly, comfortable for overnight travel.
- **Cons:** Subject to availability; longer travel time than flying.

### 3. Road (Sleeper Bus/Cab via RedBus/MMT)
- **Pros:** Flexible timing, multiple boarding points in Mumbai.
- **Cons:** Road fatigue, variable comfort levels.

## Final Recommendation
Given the budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train (3AC)** is the optimal balance of cost, comfort, and experience. If time is a priority, the **Flight** remains within budget but leaves little for local spending.
