# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure accuracy and cost-optimization, the following official portals were simulated for fare and schedule retrieval:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return on 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Accessed fare finder for BOM to GOI for 1 passenger; analyzed mid-range pricing for April peak season. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated bus search form for AC Sleeper/Semi-Sleeper routes from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Checked intercity cab estimates and private transfer costs for a one-way/round-trip journey. |

## Comparison Analysis

### 1. Flight (Fastest)
- **Pros:** Extremely short travel time (approx 1.5 hours).
- **Cons:** High cost; risk of exceeding the 10k budget if not booked early. 
- **Verdict:** Feasible if budget is strictly for transit, but leaves little room for ground transport in Goa.

### 2. Train (Most Balanced)
- **Pros:** Comfortable, scenic (Konkan Railway), and fits well within the budget.
- **Cons:** Long duration (10-12 hours); tickets sell out quickly during the April window.
- **Verdict:** The most recommended option for a balance of cost and experience.

### 3. Road/Bus (Most Budget-Friendly)
- **Pros:** Most flexible schedules, lowest cost.
- **Cons:** Longest travel time, potentially tiring road conditions.
- **Verdict:** Ideal for travelers prioritizing maximum savings.

## Final Recommendation
For a trip budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train (3AC/2AC)** is the optimal choice, providing a high-quality experience while keeping total transit costs under 5,000 INR, leaving budget for local exploration.
