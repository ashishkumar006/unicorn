# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes three primary tourist attractions in Goa, categorized by their appeal: Nature, History, and Relaxation. Data was cross-referenced via tourism boards and geocoding services to provide accurate scheduling and cost estimates.

## Detailed Comparison

| Attraction | Category | Entry Fee | Time Needed | Rating | Vibe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | Variable (Jeep + Entry) | 4-6 Hours | 4.6 | Adventurous |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | Nominal | 2 Hours | 4.2 | Cultural |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | Free | 2-3 Hours | 4.4 | Serene |

## Operational Insights
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: Requires significant planning. Entry depends on Jeep safari availability and forest department permits. Peak crowds occur between 10 AM and 2 PM.
- **Fort Aguada**: High footfall during sunset hours. Ticket structures are generally flat-rate for adults/children.
- **Velsao Beach**: A low-density alternative to North Goa beaches. No ticketing required; best visited in the early morning or late afternoon.
