# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This research report provides a detailed analysis of three primary landmarks in Goa, spanning natural wonders and historical fortifications. The selection balances the high-energy nature of Dudhsagar Falls with the coastal historical significance of Fort Aguada and Cabo de Rama.

## Detailed Findings

### 🌿 Nature & Landscapes
**Dudhsagar Falls**
- **Analysis:** A four-tiered waterfall. Access requires a jeep safari through the Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary. 
- **Crowd Metric:** Peak occupancy occurs between 10:00 AM and 1:00 PM. Early morning slots are highly recommended to avoid queueing for jeeps.
- **Ticketing:** Requires booking for the forest department jeep and entry permits.

### 🏰 History & Architecture
**Fort Aguada**
- **Analysis:** A 17th-century Portuguese fort. Known for its lighthouse and bastion.
- **Crowd Metric:** High tourist density due to proximity to Candolim beach. Best visited at sunrise.
- **Ticketing:** Standard ASI (Archaeological Survey of India) entry fees apply.

**Cabo de Rama Fort**
- **Analysis:** A remote fort located in South Goa offering dramatic cliff-side views of the Arabian Sea.
- **Crowd Metric:** Low to Moderate. Much quieter than North Goa sites.
- **Ticketing:** Minimal entry fee; primarily self-guided exploration.
