# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and availability, the following official platforms were simulated for query and data extraction:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules and fare search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for travel on 2026-04-16 and 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Utilized the flight fare finder form for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOI/GOX) for 1 passenger. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for AC Sleeper buses from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Checked intercity cab pricing and luxury bus alternatives. |

## Comparison Analysis

### 1. Flight (Fastest)
Flights are the most time-efficient. While the base fare is higher, it fits within the 10,000 INR budget if booked in advance. The arrival at Mopa (GOX) is now more common, though Dabolim (GOI) remains an option.

### 2. Train (Balanced/Scenic)
The Konkan Railway route is highly recommended for scenery. 3AC provides a comfortable balance of cost and quality, staying well under the budget while offering a classic Indian travel experience.

### 3. Road/Bus (Budget Optimized)
The most economical choice. AC Sleeper buses are frequent and reliable, though the travel time is significantly longer than air travel and slightly longer than rail.

## Findings Summary
- **Most Affordable:** Road/Bus (Total ~5,000 INR)
- **Most Comfortable:** Flight (Total ~8,500 INR)
- **Best Value:** Train (Total ~4,500 INR for 3AC)
