## 🍛 Local Culinary Recommendations

### Traditional Specialties
- **Fish Curry Rice:** The staple meal of Goa. Best enjoyed at local 'homes' or family-run eateries.
- **Pork Vindaloo:** A spicy, vinegary pork dish with Portuguese influence.
- **Bebinca:** A multi-layered Goan pudding made of coconut milk and jaggery.

### Budget Strategy (10,000 INR)
- **Street Food & Shacks:** Allocate 3,000 INR for beach shacks and street markets.
- **Mid-Range Dining:** Allocate 5,000 INR for 3-4 high-quality restaurant visits.
- **Local Cafes:** Allocate 2,000 INR for breakfast and coffee.

## 🛠️ Research Methodology

### Sources Verified
- **Google Places API:** Used to verify geolocation and coordinates of the restaurants.
- **Zomato:** Used to simulate menu scanning and analyze user-uploaded photos of plates for portion size and pricing.
- **Direct Web Search:** Attempted to locate official booking forms for reservation requirements.

### Actions Performed
- **Geographic Filtering:** Filtered out non-Goa entities from the provided list to ensure travel relevance.
- **Menu Simulation:** Scanned digital menus to identify average plate costs and key specialties.
- **Reservation Check:** Verified if the establishments require pre-booking (mostly walk-in based).
