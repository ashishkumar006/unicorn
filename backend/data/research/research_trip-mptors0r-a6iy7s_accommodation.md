## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (7 Days)

- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Detailed Analysis

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Analysis:** This property is the most viable option given the strict total trip budget of 10,000 INR. At approximately 500 INR/night, the total accommodation cost would be 3,500 INR, leaving 6,500 INR for food and activities.
- **Verdict:** Budget-friendly, high rating, and fits within the financial constraints.

### 2. The Fern Habitat Goa, Candolim (Marriott)
- **Analysis:** At 1,300 INR/night, the total cost for 6 nights is 7,800 INR. While this fits the budget, it leaves only 2,200 INR for the entire week's expenses, which is impractical for a "semi-luxury" experience.
- **Verdict:** Feasible for lodging, but severely limits other trip activities.

### 3. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Analysis:** At 2,100 INR/night, the total cost is 12,600 INR. This exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR before accounting for any other expenses.
- **Verdict:** Not viable based on the provided budget.
