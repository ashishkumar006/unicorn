# Transit Options: Mumbai to Goa

## ✈️ Option 1: Flight (The Fast Track)
- **Carrier:** IndiGo / Air India
- **Route:** BOM $\rightarrow$ GOX (Mopa) / GOI
- **Cost:** ₹4,500 - ₹6,000
- **Duration:** 1h 15m
- **Best for:** Travelers prioritizing time and comfort.

## 🚆 Option 2: Train (The Scenic Route)
- **Carrier:** Indian Railways (Konkan Railway)
- **Route:** CSMT/LTT $\rightarrow$ Madgaon (MAO)
- **Cost:** ₹1,200 - ₹2,500
- **Duration:** 12h - 15h
- **Best for:** Budget travelers and nature enthusiasts.

## 🚗 Option 3: Intercity Cab/Bus (The Flexible Way)
- **Carrier:** Private Operator / KSRTC
- **Route:** Mumbai $\rightarrow$ Panjim/Calangute
- **Cost:** ₹2,500 - ₹5,000 (Shared/Sleeper)
- **Duration:** 10h - 12h
- **Best for:** Door-to-door connectivity and flexibility.
