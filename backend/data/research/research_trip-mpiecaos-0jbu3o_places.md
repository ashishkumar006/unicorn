# Goa Attractions Guide

## 🏖️ Beaches
- **Calangute Beach**: The 'Queen of Beaches', known for its vibrant atmosphere and water sports.
- **Palolem Beach**: A crescent-shaped paradise ideal for kayaking and relaxation.
- **Baga Beach**: Famous for nightlife, shacks, and party crowds.

## 🏰 History & Architecture
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus**: A UNESCO World Heritage site holding the remains of St. Francis Xavier.
- **Aguada Fort**: 17th-century Portuguese fort offering panoramic views of the Arabian Sea.
- **Fort Chapora**: Historic fort famous for its connection to popular Indian cinema and stunning cliff views.

## 🌿 Nature & Culture
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A massive four-tiered waterfall known as the 'Sea of Milk'.
- **Mangrove Walk**: Peaceful nature trails through Goa's coastal ecosystems.
- **Spice Plantations**: Immersive experiences learning about tropical spice cultivation.

### 💡 Travel Tips
- **Transport**: Renting a scooter (Activa/Vespa) is the most efficient way to navigate.
- **Timing**: Visit between November and February for the best weather.
- **Dress Code**: Dress modestly when visiting churches and temples.
