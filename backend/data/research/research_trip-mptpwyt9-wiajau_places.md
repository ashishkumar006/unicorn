# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Overview
This research report provides a detailed analysis of top tourist landmarks in Goa, focusing on natural wonders, historical fortifications, and serene coastlines. We have evaluated these sites based on accessibility, cost, and visitor experience.

## Comparative Analysis

| Attraction | Category | Rating | Time Required | Primary Appeal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | 4.6 | 1-2 Hours (Site Visit) | Majestic waterfalls and jungle trekking |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | 4.2 | 1-2 Hours | Portuguese architecture and ocean views |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | 4.4 | 1-2 Hours | Peace, quiet, and pristine sands |

## Detailed Findings

### 🌿 Nature & Wildlife
**Dudhsagar Falls** remains the top-rated attraction. Due to its remote location, it requires significant travel time via Jeep. Simulation of entry forms indicates that pricing varies based on the season (monsoon vs. winter).

### 🏰 History & Culture
**Fort Aguada** is a cornerstone of Goan history. It offers a blend of military architecture and scenic vistas. Entry is generally accessible, though crowd metrics peak between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM.

### 🏖️ Coastal Relaxation
**Velsao Beach** serves as a tranquil alternative to the crowded beaches of North Goa. It is ideal for those seeking a slow-paced experience.

## Research Methodology
- **Data Retrieval:** Cross-referenced Google Places API for ratings and OpenStreetMap for precise geocoding.
- **Verification:** Simulated checks of the Goa Tourism Board's official portal to validate entry structures.
- **Occupancy Analysis:** Analyzed historical crowd patterns to determine optimal visiting windows.
