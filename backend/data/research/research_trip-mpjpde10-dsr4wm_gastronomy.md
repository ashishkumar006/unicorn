# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Culinary Exploration

## Executive Summary
Based on the provided data and local research, the primary focus for a budget of ₹10,000 is to balance authentic Goan seafood and regional delicacies with affordable dining. Note: Initial search results contained outliers (Delhi and MP); these were filtered out to ensure the itinerary remains within the state of Goa.

## Restaurant Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant (Mapusa)
- **Analysis**: A highly-rated local spot catering to travelers and residents. It offers a balanced price-to-quality ratio, making it ideal for the provided budget.
- **Budget Fit**: Excellent. Average plate cost allows for multiple high-quality meals without exhausting the ₹10,000 limit.
- **Vibe**: Casual dining with a focus on regional flavors.

## Local Culinary Specialties
To maximize the gastronomic experience, the traveler should explore beyond restaurants:
- **Goan Fish Curry**: A staple found in almost every local eatery.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional cake.
- **Ros Omelette**: A popular street-side breakfast/snack.
