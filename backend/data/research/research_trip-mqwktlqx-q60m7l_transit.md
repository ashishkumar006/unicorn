# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Executive Summary
Research conducted for a round trip from Mumbai to Goa for one traveler (2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22). The primary objective was to stay within the 10,000 INR budget while balancing travel time and comfort.

## Source & Action Log
| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules and fares for Mumbai CSMT to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked flight fare finder for BOM to GOI/GOX for passenger count: 1 on specified dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper buses from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Compared intercity cab rates and luxury bus options for the specified route. |

## Transport Comparison

### 1. Flight (Fastest)
- **Pros**: Extremely short travel time, maximum convenience.
- **Cons**: Most expensive option; can consume 60-80% of the total budget.
- **Findings**: Flight prices fluctuate; booking early for April 2026 suggests a range of 4,500 - 6,500 INR round trip.

### 2. Train (Balanced)
- **Pros**: Scenic Konkan Railway route, comfortable for overnight travel, very budget-friendly.
- **Cons**: Subject to availability/waiting lists during peak seasons.
- **Findings**: The Tejas Express and Konkan Kanya Express provide excellent connectivity. 3AC fares are well within budget.

### 3. Road/Bus (Most Economical)
- **Pros**: Most frequent departures, flexible timings, lowest cost.
- **Cons**: Longest travel time, potential for traffic on the Ghats.
- **Findings**: AC Sleeper buses are the preferred road choice, offering a balance of cost and comfort.

## Final Recommendation
For a budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train (3AC)** is the recommended option as it leaves ample room for local exploration and accommodation while providing a scenic and comfortable journey.
