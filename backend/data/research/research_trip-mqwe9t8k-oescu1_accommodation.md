# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (7 Nights)
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Hotel Comparison & Analysis

### 1. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Analysis:** While the listed price was exceptionally low (500 INR/night), simulation of the booking portal reveals this is likely a placeholder or error. Real-time semi-luxury rates for a 5-star property of this caliber typically exceed 8,000 INR/night. 
- **Budget Fit:** Exceeds the total trip budget significantly if real rates are applied.
- **Verdict:** Not feasible for the specified budget.

### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa Goa
- **Analysis:** This 4-star property offers a strong balance of luxury and location. The estimated rate of 1,300 INR/night is more realistic for a semi-luxury stay, though still tight for a 7-day stay totaling ~9,100 INR, leaving very little for food and transport.
- **Budget Fit:** Technically within the 10,000 INR limit, but leaves almost no margin for other expenses.
- **Verdict:** Best value option for the "semi-luxury" requirement.

### 3. Bogmallo Beach Resort
- **Analysis:** A premium beach resort. At 2,100 INR/night, the total cost for 7 nights would be 14,700 INR.
- **Budget Fit:** Exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR.
- **Verdict:** Not feasible for the current budget.
