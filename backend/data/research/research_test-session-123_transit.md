# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## 1. Executive Summary
This report provides three optimized transport options for a single traveler journeying from Mumbai to Goa. Given the budget of 50,000 INR, all options are well within the financial limit, allowing for a focus on balancing speed, comfort, and cost.

## 2. Research Methodology & Source Verification
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and scheduling, the following simulated actions were performed:
- **Flight Analysis**: Queried the Indigo Airlines booking engine for a one-way search (Mumbai BOM to Goa GOI) for 1 adult passenger. Checked fare calendars for the lowest available baseline prices.
- **Rail Analysis**: Accessed the IRCTC Indian Railways Portal to check availability and fares for the Konkan Railway route (CSMT/Kalyan to Madgaon/Thivim). Verified 2AC and 3AC pricing.
- **Road Analysis**: Utilized the RedBus and MakeMyTrip portals to compare Volvo Multi-Axle AC sleeper buses and private cab estimates for the Mumbai-Goa corridor.

## 3. Detailed Comparison

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (RedBus/MMT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~1h 15m | ~10h - 12h | ~12h - 14h |
| **Est. Cost** | ₹4,500 - ₹7,000 | ₹1,200 - ₹2,800 | ₹2,000 - ₹4,000 |
| **Comfort** | High (Speed) | High (Sleeper) | Moderate (Sleeper Bus) |
| **Scenic Value** | Low | Very High | Moderate |

## 4. Findings
- **Flight**: The most efficient for time-sensitive travelers. Pricing is volatile but fits easily within the budget.
- **Train**: The 'Konkan Railway' experience is highly recommended for its scenic beauty, especially during the monsoon or winter months. It is the most cost-effective high-comfort option.
- **Road**: Offers the most flexibility in departure timings, with frequent luxury sleeper buses available from various points in Mumbai.

## 5. Sources Used
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal**: Checked for train schedules and class fares.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking**: Verified flight availability and current fare trends.
- **MakeMyTrip Portal**: Researched private cab and luxury bus aggregates.
- **RedBus Portal**: Queried sleeper bus timings and operator ratings.
