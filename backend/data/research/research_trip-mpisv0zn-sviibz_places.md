# 🌴 Comprehensive Guide to Goa's Top Attractions

## 🏖️ Beaches & Coastal Sights
- **Calangute & Baga**: The heartbeat of North Goa. High energy, water sports, and night markets.
- **Palolem**: A serene crescent-shaped beach in the South, perfect for kayaking and quiet retreats.

## 🏛️ History & Architecture
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus**: A UNESCO World Heritage site holding the remains of St. Francis Xavier.
- **Aguada Fort**: 17th-century Portuguese fort offering panoramic views of the Arabian Sea.

## 🌿 Nature & Wellness
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A majestic four-tiered waterfall. Best visited via Jeep safari.
- **Spice Plantations**: Immersive tours of cardamom, vanilla, and pepper groves in Ponda.

### 💡 Pro Travel Tips:
1. **Transport**: Rent a scooty or Thar for the best flexibility.
2. **Timing**: Visit between November and February for ideal weather.
3. **Dress Code**: Modest clothing is required for churches and temples.
