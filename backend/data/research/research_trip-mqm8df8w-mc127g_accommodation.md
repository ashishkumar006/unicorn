### Detailed Findings

#### 1. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Findings:** This property offers the best balance of luxury and cost for the specified budget. While the base rate is listed low, simulation shows standard rooms for single occupancy are available. The "Semi-Luxury" requirement is well-met by the 5-star status.
- **Pros:** High rating, luxury brand, fits within the 10k INR total budget.
- **Cons:** High demand may cause price fluctuations closer to 2026.

#### 2. Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim, Goa
- **Findings:** A solid 4-star option. However, at 1,300 INR/night, the total comes to 9,100 INR, leaving almost no room in the 10,000 INR budget for food, transport, or activities.
- **Pros:** Great location in Candolim, consistent brand quality.
- **Cons:** Pushes the total trip budget to the limit.

#### 3. FabHotel Grand Royal Palms
- **Findings:** At 2,100 INR/night, this option exceeds the total trip budget (14,700 INR total), making it unfeasible for this specific request.
- **Pros:** Spacious rooms.
- **Cons:** Price exceeds budget; lower rating than competitors.
