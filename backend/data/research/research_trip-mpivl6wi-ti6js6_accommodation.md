### Findings

#### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Analysis:** The most economical option. While categorized as 4-star, the price point is extremely low, leaving significant room in the budget for other activities. Ideal for the 'semi-luxury' feel if the traveler prioritizes spending on dining and excursions.
- **Suitability:** Highly suitable for budget adherence.

#### 2. Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim, Goa
- **Analysis:** This hotel hits the 'semi-luxury' sweet spot. It provides a branded experience with reliable quality and amenities while remaining within the 10,000 INR cap.
- **Suitability:** Recommended as the primary choice for balancing luxury and budget.

#### 3. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Analysis:** While offering premium services and a high rating, the total cost for 6 nights (approx. 12,600 INR) exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR.
- **Suitability:** Not feasible unless budget is increased.
