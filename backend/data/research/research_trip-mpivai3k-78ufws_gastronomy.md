## Detailed Analysis
### Spice Goa Restaurant
- **Analysis**: Located on the Mumbai-Goa Highway, this spot is ideal for travelers entering the state. It offers a wide range of regional curries and seafood.
- **Budget Impact**: At ₹500 per head, it allows for multiple high-quality meals without depleting the 10,000 INR budget.
- **Recommendation**: Best for an authentic lunch during transit or a hearty dinner.

## Regional Culinary Guide
### Local Specialties
- **Fish Curry Rice**: The staple of every Goan household.
- **Bebinca**: A layered Goan pudding made from coconut milk and eggs.
- **Xacuti**: A rich, spicy curry made with roasted coconut and spices.
