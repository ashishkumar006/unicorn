# Transit Analysis: Mumbai to Goa

## ✈️ Option 1: Flight (Fastest)
- **Carrier:** IndiGo
- **Route:** BOM $\rightarrow$ GOX (Manohar International Airport)
- **Price:** ₹4,200 - ₹6,500
- **Duration:** 1h 15m
- **Pros:** Maximum time saved, modern terminal.
- **Cons:** Airport is further from North Goa beaches.

## 🚂 Option 2: Train (Most Scenic)
- **Train:** Konkan Railway / Tejas Express
- **Route:** CSMT/Dadarl $\rightarrow$ Madgaon
- **Price:** ₹1,200 (3AC) - ₹2,800 (CC)
- **Duration:** 10h - 12h
- **Pros:** Stunning Western Ghats views, budget-friendly.
- **Cons:** Long travel time, tickets sell out fast.

## 🚕 Option 3: Intercity Cab/Bus (Flexible)
- **Type:** Private AC Cab / Volvo Bus
- **Route:** Mumbai $\rightarrow$ Panaji/Calangute
- **Price:** ₹3,500 (Bus) - ₹9,000 (Private Cab)
- **Duration:** 12h - 14h
- **Pros:** Door-to-door service, flexible stops.
- **Cons:** Exhausting journey, traffic at ghats.
