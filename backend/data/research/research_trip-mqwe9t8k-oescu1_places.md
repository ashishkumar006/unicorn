# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report provides a detailed analysis of top-rated landmarks in Goa, ranging from colonial fortifications to natural waterfalls. The research focuses on accessibility, pricing, and time management for travelers.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Natural Wonders
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A massive four-tiered waterfall. Due to its remote location, transport (Jeeps) is mandatory. High crowd metrics observed during peak morning hours (8 AM - 11 AM).
- **Kesarval Spring**: A more serene, less crowded alternative to Dudhsagar, best for those seeking tranquility over spectacle.

### 2. Historical Landmarks
- **Fort Aguada**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort. It offers panoramic views of the Arabian Sea. Crowd metrics indicate peak visiting times between 4 PM and 6 PM for sunsets.

## Operational Logistics & Ticketing
- **Ticketing**: Dudhsagar requires a combination of forest department entry fees and private jeep hire. Fort Aguada utilizes a standard nominal entry ticket.
- **Crowd Management**: Higher occupancy is noted at Fort Aguada during weekends. Dudhsagar is subject to seasonal closures (Monsoon).

## Research Methodology
- **Sources Used**: Google Places API, Goa Tourism Board, OpenStreetMap.
- **Actions Performed**: Simulated verification of ticket booking forms for forest reserves, analyzed hourly popularity trends via Google Maps API, and cross-referenced entry fee structures on state tourism portals.
