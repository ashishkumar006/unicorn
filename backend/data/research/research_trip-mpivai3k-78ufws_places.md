# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes three primary attractions in Goa, ranging from natural wonders to historical fortifications and serene beaches. The selection provides a balanced mix of activity levels and thematic interests.

## Detailed Comparison

| Attraction | Category | Key Appeal | Effort Level | Estimated Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | Dramatic waterfalls, jungle trek | High | Moderate (Jeep hire) |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | Portuguese architecture, ocean views | Low | Low |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | Quiet atmosphere, white sands | Low | Free |

## Operational Insights
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: Requires advanced planning due to forest department permits and jeep requirements. Peak crowds occur between 10 AM and 2 PM.
- **Fort Aguada**: High footfall during sunset hours. Entry is generally straightforward with minimal ticketing friction.
- **Velsao Beach**: A non-commercialized spot, making it ideal for those avoiding crowds typical of Baga or Calangute.
