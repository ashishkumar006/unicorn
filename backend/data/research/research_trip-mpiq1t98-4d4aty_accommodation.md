Analyzing three selected properties for a **single traveler** with a budget of **10,000 INR** and a **semi-luxury** preference. All listed properties are 4-star rated, fitting the luxury requirement, while remaining well within the total budget.

## 🔍 Detailed Breakdown

### 🌟 The LaLiT Golf & Spa Resort Goa
*The pinnacle of the selection, offering a true semi-to-full luxury experience.*
- **Best For:** Travelers seeking wellness, golf, and high-end aesthetics.
- **Key Highlight:** Superior rating and luxury resort infrastructure.
- **Budget Impact:** Lowest efficiency but highest quality (Approx ₹2,100/night).
- [Official Website](https://www.thelalit.com/the-lalit-goa/)

### 🍋 Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim
*A balanced choice combining professional hospitality with a prime location.*
- **Best For:** Those wanting proximity to Candolim's beaches and nightlife.
- **Key Highlight:** Standardized 4-star quality and consistent service.
- **Budget Impact:** Mid-range (Approx ₹1,300/night).
- [Official Website](https://www.lemontreehotels.com/lemon-tree-hotel/goa/candolim-goa?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gmb_listing)

### 🌿 Shelsta Holiday Resort
*The most economical 4-star option available.*
- **Best For:** Maximizing the trip budget for activities and dining.
- **Key Highlight:** Incredible affordability while maintaining a 4-star rating.
- **Budget Impact:** Highly efficient (Approx ₹500/night).
- [Official Website](http://www.shelstaholidayresort.com/)

## 💡 Final Recommendation
For a **semi-luxury** experience, **The LaLiT Golf & Spa Resort** is the top choice. Even at ₹2,100/night, it remains far below the 10,000 INR budget, allowing the traveler to enjoy the highest level of amenity and comfort available in this list.
