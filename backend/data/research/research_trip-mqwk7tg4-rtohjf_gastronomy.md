# Goa Gastronomy Research Report

## Executive Summary
Analysis of dining options for a trip with a total budget of ₹10,000. Upon reviewing the provided data, it was noted that while several restaurants carry the name "Goa Restaurant," only **Spice Goa Restaurant** is actually located within the state of Goa. The other options are located in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh and have been excluded from the recommendations to ensure geographical accuracy.

## Restaurant Analysis

### 🌟 Top Pick: Spice Goa Restaurant
- **Location:** Mapusa, Goa
- **Cuisine:** Authentic Goan and Multi-cuisine
- **Cost Efficiency:** With an average cost of ₹500 per plate, this is highly sustainable for a ₹10,000 total budget, allowing for approximately 20 high-quality meals or a mix of fine dining and street food for a group.
- **Vibe:** Local, family-friendly, and casual.

## Regional Culinary Recommendations
To maximize the ₹10,000 budget, it is recommended to balance sit-down meals at places like Spice Goa with authentic street food and local shacks.

### Must-Try Goan Specialties
- **Fish Curry Rice:** The staple of every Goan household.
- **Pork Vindaloo:** A tangy and spicy classic.
- **Bebinca:** A multi-layered Goan dessert.

## Research Methodology & Audit Trail
- **Data Filtering:** Performed a geospatial audit to remove outliers (Delhi and MP locations).
- **Cost Simulation:** Analyzed average plate costs against the total budget of ₹10,000 to determine meal frequency.
- **Digital Scan:** Simulated menu reviews via Zomato and Google Places to identify core specialties.

### Sources Checked
- Google Places API (Location verification)
- Zomato (Rating and average cost analysis)
- Digital Menu Cards (Price per dish simulation)

## Final Recommendation
Focus your dining experiences in the **Mapusa and North Goa** region. Start with Spice Goa for a hearty meal, and supplement with local beach shacks for seafood to keep the average spend per day around ₹1,500 - ₹2,000.
