# Accommodation Research Report: Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (6 nights)
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR total
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Detailed Hotel Analysis

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Status:** Highly Recommended (Budget Friendly)
- **Simulated Rate:** ~500 INR/night
- **Total Stay Cost:** ~3,000 INR
- **Findings:** This property is the most economically viable option, leaving significant room in the budget for activities and dining. It offers a balanced semi-luxury feel for the price point.
- **Verdict:** Best for maximizing the overall trip budget.

### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa Goa
- **Status:** Budget Constraint Risk
- **Simulated Rate:** ~1,300 INR/night
- **Total Stay Cost:** ~7,800 INR
- **Findings:** While this is a high-quality 4-star experience, the total cost for 6 nights consumes nearly 78% of the total trip budget (10,000 INR), leaving very little for food, transport, or experiences.
- **Verdict:** High quality, but potentially impractical for the total budget.

### 3. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Status:** Exceeds Budget
- **Simulated Rate:** ~2,100 INR/night
- **Total Stay Cost:** ~12,600 INR
- **Findings:** The cost of accommodation alone exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR. Despite the 5-star luxury, it is not financially feasible for this specific constraint.
- **Verdict:** Not recommended due to budget limitations.
