# Goa Gastronomy Research Report

## Executive Summary
Following a rigorous analysis of available dining data for the region, it is important to note that only one of the provided options is actually located within the state of Goa. The other listings are located in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh and have been excluded from the final dietary recommendations to maintain regional accuracy.

## Selected Dining Analysis
### 🏆 Top Recommendation: Spice Goa Restaurant
Located strategically on the Mumbai-Goa Highway, this establishment offers an authentic blend of regional flavors. It fits perfectly within the ₹10,000 total trip budget, allowing for multiple high-quality meals without financial strain.

- **Cuisine Profile**: Traditional Goan and Multi-cuisine.
- **Price Point**: Budget-friendly (~₹500 per person).
- **Vibe**: Casual, traveler-friendly, and authentic.

## Regional Culinary Guide
To maximize the budget of ₹10,000, I recommend a mix of sit-down restaurant meals and exploring local shacks and street markets.

### Must-Try Local Specialties
- **Fish Curry Rice**: The staple of every Goan household.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional Goan cake.
- **Xacuti**: A complex, spicy curry made with grated coconut.

## Budget Allocation Strategy
- **Fine Dining/Restaurants**: 40% (₹4,000)
- **Beach Shacks/Cafes**: 30% (₹3,000)
- **Street Food & Snacks**: 30% (₹3,000)
