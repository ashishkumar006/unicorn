# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip (April 2026)

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1 Adult
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (6 Nights)
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR (Total Trip)
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Detailed Comparison & Findings

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Analysis:** This is the only option that realistically fits within the total trip budget. With a rate of ~500 INR per night, the total stay costs approximately 3,000 INR, leaving 7,000 INR for food and transport.
- **Value:** High budget efficiency, though it falls on the lower end of the "semi-luxury" spectrum.
- **Verdict:** Recommended for budget adherence.

### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa Goa, Panjim
- **Analysis:** At 1,300 INR per night, the 6-night stay totals 7,800 INR. While this fits the accommodation budget, it leaves only 2,200 INR for all other expenses for 7 days, which is likely insufficient for a "semi-luxury" experience.
- **Value:** Mid-range luxury with excellent amenities.
- **Verdict:** Feasible only if other trip expenses are heavily subsidized.

### 3. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Analysis:** At 2,100 INR per night, the total stay is 12,600 INR, which exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR before any other spending.
- **Value:** High luxury, but financially non-viable for this specific budget.
- **Verdict:** Rejected due to budget constraints.

## Research Methodology

### Sources Checked
- **Google Places API:** Used for rating validation, coordinate confirmation, and image sourcing.
- **Official Portals:** Shelsta Holiday Resort, Marriott (The Fern), and Hard Rock Hotels official booking engines.

### Actions Performed
- **Form Simulation:** Populated 'Check-in Date' (2026-04-16), 'Check-out Date' (2026-04-22), and 'Guests' (1 Adult).
- **Occupancy Validation:** Verified that single-occupancy rates were applied rather than standard double-room rates.
- **Rate Cross-Referencing:** Compared listed price per night against total stay calculations to ensure budget alignment.
