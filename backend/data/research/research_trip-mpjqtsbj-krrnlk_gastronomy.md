## Dining Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant (Recommended)
Located on the Mumbai-Goa Highway, this establishment serves as an excellent mid-range anchor for the trip. With an average cost of ₹500 per person, it allows for multiple high-quality meals without exhausting the 10k budget.
- **Vibe:** Casual, family-oriented, transit-friendly.
- **Budget Impact:** Moderate (approx. 5-10% of total trip budget per visit).

## Local Culinary Specialties

- **Goan Fish Thali:** A staple consisting of rice, fish curry, fried fish, and vegetables.
- **Bebinca:** The traditional multi-layered Goan cake.
