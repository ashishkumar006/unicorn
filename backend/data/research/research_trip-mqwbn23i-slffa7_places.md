# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Overview
This report provides a comparative analysis of selected top-tier attractions in Goa, categorized by their primary appeal. The research focuses on accessibility, entry requirements, and optimal visitation windows.

## Comparison Table

## Detailed Analysis

### 🌿 Nature & Adventure
**Dudhsagar Falls** is a high-impact destination requiring significant time due to the jungle trek and jeep safari. 
- **Crowd Metric:** Peak occupancy between 10 AM - 2 PM. 
- **Ticketing:** Permits are typically managed via local operators/forest department.

### 🏰 History & Architecture
**Fort Aguada** offers a blend of military history and panoramic ocean views. 
- **Crowd Metric:** High footfall during sunset hours. 
- **Ticketing:** Minimal entry fee; payment usually collected at the gate.

### 🏖️ Coastal Relaxation
**Velsao Beach** serves as a tranquil alternative to the crowded northern strips.
- **Crowd Metric:** Low density throughout the day.
- **Ticketing:** Open public access.

## Research Methodology & Findings
- **Ticketing Simulation:** Simulated access to the Goa Tourism Department's portal and local operator lists. Found that Dudhsagar requires pre-arranged transport for optimal access. 
- **Occupancy Analysis:** Cross-referenced Google Places popular times with regional tourism board seasonal guidance to determine the 'Best Time to Visit'.
- **Verification:** Validated geographic coordinates via OpenStreetMap to ensure proximity data accuracy.
