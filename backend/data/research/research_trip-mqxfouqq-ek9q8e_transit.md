# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and scheduling, the following actions were performed:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked Indigo flight fare finder for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOX/GOI) for 1 passenger. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Simulated cab/intercity search form for one-way and round-trip private transfers. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Queried bus schedules for luxury sleeper coaches between Mumbai and Goa. |

## Comparison Analysis

### 1. Flight (The Speed Option)
Flights are the fastest but most expensive. While a round trip can fit within the 10,000 INR budget if booked early, prices fluctuate. The new Manohar International Airport (GOX) offers better connectivity.

### 2. Train (The Value Option)
The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC or Sleeper class is the most budget-friendly way to travel, leaving significant room in the budget for local experiences in Goa.

### 3. Road/Cab (The Flexible Option)
Private cabs are prohibitively expensive for a single traveler (often exceeding 15,000 INR round trip). Therefore, a luxury AC Sleeper Bus via RedBus is the optimized 'Road' recommendation to stay within budget.

## Final Recommendations
- **Best for Budget:** Train (Sleeper/3AC)
- **Best for Time:** Flight (Indigo)
- **Best for Scenery:** Train (Konkan Railway)
