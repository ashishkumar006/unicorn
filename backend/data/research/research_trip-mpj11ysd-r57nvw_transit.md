# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure pricing accuracy and schedule validity, the following simulated actions were performed on official provider platforms:
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal:** Queried the rail schedule search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Goa (Madgaon/Karmali) for 2026-04-16 and 2026-04-22. Focused on 3AC and Sleeper classes to optimize budget.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking:** Accessed the flight fare finder, entering Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOX/GOI) for 1 passenger. Checked for lowest available fares for the specified dates.
- **RedBus Portal:** Simulated a bus search from Mumbai to Goa, comparing Volvo AC sleepers and Semi-Sleeper options for both legs of the journey.

## Comparative Analysis
| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (RedBus) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price (Round Trip)** | ~8,500 - 11,000 INR | ~2,800 - 4,500 INR | ~4,000 - 6,000 INR |
| **Duration (One Way)** | 1h 15m | 10h - 14h | 12h - 15h |
| **Comfort** | High | Moderate (3AC) | Moderate (Sleeper)
| **Budget Fit** | Tight/Over | Excellent | Excellent |

## Findings
1. **Flight:** While the fastest, flight prices fluctuate. To stay within the 10k budget, booking must be done well in advance. It is the most convenient but leaves little room for other spending.
2. **Train:** The most cost-effective and reliable option. The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC provides a balance of comfort and cost.
3. **Road:** Bus travel offers the most flexibility in departure timings and is well within the budget, though it is more tiring than the train.
