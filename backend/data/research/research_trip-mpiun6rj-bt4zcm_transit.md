# Mumbai to Goa Transport Report

## ✈️ Option 1: Flight (Fastest)
- **Carrier:** IndiGo
- **Duration:** 1h 15m
- **Price:** ₹4,500 - ₹6,000
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, time-efficient.
- **Cons:** Airport transfers add to time and cost.

## 🚂 Option 2: Train (Most Scenic)
- **Train:** Konkan Railway / Tejas Express
- **Duration:** 11h 30m
- **Price:** ₹1,200 - ₹2,500
- **Pros:** Stunning views of the Western Ghats, budget-friendly.
- **Cons:** Longer travel time.

## 🚗 Option 3: Intercity Cab/Bus (Flexible)
- **Service:** Private Cab/Luxury Bus
- **Duration:** 12h - 14h
- **Price:** ₹3,000 - ₹7,000
- **Pros:** Door-to-door service, flexible stops.
- **Cons:** Tiring journey, dependent on traffic.

--- 
**Budget Analysis:** All options fall well within the ₹10,000 limit, allowing for luxury upgrades or local transport within Goa.
