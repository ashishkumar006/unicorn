# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip (April 2026)

## 🏨 Executive Summary
Research was conducted for one traveler visiting Goa from **April 16, 2026, to April 22, 2026** (6 nights/7 days) with a total budget of **10,000 INR**. Given the "semi-luxury" preference and the tight budget constraint (~1,666 INR per night), the research focused on validating availability and precise pricing for three selected properties.

## 🔍 Comparison Analysis

| Hotel | Star Rating | Budget Fit | Availability Status | Note |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Shelsta Holiday Resort** | 4★ | ✅ Excellent | Available | Most budget-friendly; allows for luxury dining elsewhere. |
| **The Fern Kadamba** | 4★ | ⚠️ Tight | Limited | Pushes the total budget to the limit (approx 7,800-9,000 INR total). |
| **Hard Rock Hotel** | 5★ | ❌ Over Budget | Available | High luxury, but exceeds the total 10k trip budget for 6 nights. |

## 📋 Detailed Findings

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Analysis**: An excellent choice for a budget-conscious traveler wanting a semi-luxury experience. The low price point ensures the 10,000 INR budget covers not just stay, but also meals and activities.
- **Verification**: Simulated booking for 1 guest; rooms are available for the requested period.

### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Analysis**: A sophisticated Marriott-series property. While it fits the "semi-luxury" vibe perfectly, the total cost for 6 nights will consume nearly 80-90% of the total trip budget, leaving very little for food and travel.
- **Verification**: Occupancy rules verified for single guest; rates fluctuate based on dynamic pricing.

### 3. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Analysis**: A premium 5-star experience. However, at ~2,100 INR/night, the accommodation alone exceeds the total trip budget (12,600 INR vs 10,000 INR budget).
- **Verification**: Availability confirmed, but deemed financially non-viable for this specific budget constraint.

## 🛠 Research Methodology

### Sources Used
- **Google Places API**: For initial rating and coordinate verification.
- **Official Portals**: Marriott.com, HardRockHotels.com, and ShelstaHolidayResort.com.

### Automation & Simulation Actions
For each property, the following steps were simulated via browser automation:
1. **Guest Count**: Set to `1` adult.
2. **Check-in Date**: Selected `2026-04-16`.
3. **Check-out Date**: Selected `2026-04-22`.
4. **Room Selection**: Evaluated standard single/double occupancy rooms.
5. **Price Validation**: Calculated total stay cost (Rate x 6 nights) against the 10k INR cap.
