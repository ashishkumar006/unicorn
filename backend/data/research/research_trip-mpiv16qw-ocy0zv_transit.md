# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Comparative Analysis

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (RedBus/MMT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Travel Time** | ~1h 15m | ~10h - 14h | ~12h - 15h |
| **Est. Round Trip Cost** | ₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ₹2,800 - ₹4,500 | ₹3,000 - ₹6,000 |
| **Comfort** | High | Moderate (3AC) | Moderate (Sleeper) |
| **Budget Fit** | Tight/Over | Excellent | Excellent |

## Findings
1. **Flight:** The fastest option. While prices fluctuate, early booking for April (peak-ish season) typically stays around ₹4,000-₹5,000 per leg, putting the total just at or slightly above the 10k budget.
2. **Train:** The most cost-effective and reliable option. The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC is recommended for the balance of cost and comfort.
3. **Road:** Bus travel is frequent and flexible. Sleeper coaches are the standard for this long-haul journey to ensure the traveler arrives rested.

## Final Recommendations
- **Best Value:** Train (3AC) - Highly scenic and well within budget.
- **Fastest:** Flight - If budget can be stretched by 5-10%.
- **Most Flexible:** AC Sleeper Bus - Frequent departures.
