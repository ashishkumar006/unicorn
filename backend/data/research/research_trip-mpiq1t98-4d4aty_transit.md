# Transit Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Travel Comparison
| Mode | Duration | Estimated Price | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flight** | 1h 15m | ₹4,500 - ₹6,500 | Speed & Convenience |
| **Train** | 12h 30m | ₹800 - ₹1,800 | Budget & Scenery |
| **Road/Cab** | 10h 00m | ₹8,000 - ₹12,000 | Flexibility & Groups |

### Detailed Analysis

#### 1. Aerial Route (IndiGo)
- **Pros**: Minimizes travel time, maximum productivity.
- **Cons**: Airport commute time and costs.

#### 2. Rail Route (Konkan Railway)
- **Pros**: Extremely affordable, breathtaking views of the Western Ghats.
- **Cons**: Longer duration, requires advance booking.

#### 3. Road Route (Private Cab)
- **Pros**: Door-to-door service, stopovers at Lonavala/Kolhapur.
- **Cons**: Most expensive option, tiring for the passenger.
