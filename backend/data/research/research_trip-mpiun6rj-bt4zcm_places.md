# Goa Attractions Guide

## 🏖️ Beaches
- **Calangute Beach**: The 'Queen of Beaches', ideal for water sports.
- **Palolem Beach**: A serene crescent-shaped beach in South Goa, perfect for relaxation.
- **Baga Beach**: Famous for its nightlife and beachfront shacks.

## 🏛️ History & Heritage
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus**: A UNESCO World Heritage site holding the remains of St. Francis Xavier.
- **Aguada Fort**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort with stunning ocean views.
- **Old Goa Churches**: A collection of Baroque architecture reflecting Portuguese influence.

## 🌿 Nature & Waterfalls
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A massive four-tiered waterfall known as the 'Sea of Milk'.
- **Bhagwan Mahavir Sanctuary**: A wildlife haven for trekking and birdwatching.

## 💡 Travel Tips
- **Transport**: Renting a scooter or car is the most efficient way to explore.
- **Best Time to Visit**: November to February for pleasant weather.
- **Clothing**: Light cotton clothes; modest attire is required for churches.
