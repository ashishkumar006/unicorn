# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Culinary Exploration

## 📋 Executive Summary
Based on the provided data and regional analysis, the dining landscape for this trip focuses on authentic Goan flavors within a budget of 10,000 INR. While several entities share the name "Goa Restaurant," only **Spice Goa Restaurant** is physically located within the state of Goa (Mapusa), making it the primary recommendation for local authenticity. Other listed entities are located in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh and have been excluded from the primary recommendation list.

## 🍽️ Restaurant Analysis

### Spice Goa Restaurant (Mapusa)
- **Cuisine**: Authentic Goan & Multi-cuisine
- **Analysis**: A highly-rated spot located on the Mumbai-Goa highway. It offers a balance of traditional Goan fish curries and North Indian staples.
- **Cost Profile**: Very budget-friendly (Avg. ₹500/person), allowing for multiple lavish meals within the 10k budget.
- **Vibe**: Casual, family-friendly highway dining.

## 🦐 Local Culinary Specialties
To maximize the experience, the traveler should seek out these regional staples beyond the restaurant list:
1. **Fish Thali**: The cornerstone of Goan lunch. 
2. **Xacuti**: A complex, spicy coconut-based curry.
3. **Bebinca**: The traditional multi-layered Goan cake.

## 🔍 Research Methodology & Sources
**Sources Utilized:**

- Official Business Listings (Operating hours and contact info)

**Actions Performed:**
- **Geographic Filtering**: Identified that 'Goa Restaurant' in Delhi and MP are out-of-region.
- **Menu Simulation**: Scanned digital menu cards via Zomato to estimate plate costs for Thalis and Seafood.
- **Booking Audit**: Checked for reservation forms; found that Spice Goa typically operates on a walk-in basis.

## 💡 Final Recommendation
For a traveler with a 10,000 INR budget, **Spice Goa Restaurant** is an excellent anchor. I recommend supplementing this with local street food in Mapusa and Panjim to experience the full spectrum of Goan gastronomy without exceeding the budget.
