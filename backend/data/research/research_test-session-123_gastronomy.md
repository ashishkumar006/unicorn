# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Culinary Exploration

## Executive Summary
Analysis of dining options focused on authentic Goan flavors. Based on the provided data, only **Spice Goa Restaurant** is located within the target destination (Goa), while others were filtered out as they are located in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh. To maximize a 50,000 INR budget, we recommend a blend of mid-range dining and traditional street food.

## Detailed Restaurant Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant
- **Cuisine**: Authentic Goan & Indian
- **Vibe**: Family-friendly / Casual Dining
- **Price Point**: Mid-to-High (Approx ₹2,500 for 2)
- **Verdict**: Excellent for experiencing local seafood and traditional curries in a comfortable setting.

## Local Culinary Specialties
Goa's cuisine is a unique blend of Portuguese and Konkani influences. Key highlights include:
- **Fish Curry Rice**: The staple meal of every Goan household.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional Goan dessert.
- **Pork Vindaloo**: A spicy, vinegary meat dish reflecting Portuguese heritage.

## Budgetary Recommendations
With a 50,000 INR budget, dining should occupy approximately 15-20% (₹7,500 - ₹10,000). This allows for 2-3 high-end meals at places like Spice Goa and daily exploration of beach shacks and street markets.
