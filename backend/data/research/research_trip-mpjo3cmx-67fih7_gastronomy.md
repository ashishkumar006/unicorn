### Detailed Breakdown: Spice Goa Restaurant
Located on the Mumbai-Goa Highway, this spot is ideal for travelers. It offers a balanced price-to-quality ratio, making it highly sustainable for a ₹10k budget. The menu focuses on traditional spices and fresh local ingredients.

## Regional Culinary Guide

### Must-Try Local Specialties
*   **Fish Curry Rice:** The staple of every Goan household.
*   **Pork Vindaloo:** A tangy, spicy dish reflecting Portuguese influence.
*   **Bebinca:** A multi-layered traditional Goan cake.

## Research Methodology
- **Filtering:** Removed non-Goa entities from the dataset.

- **Cross-Referencing:** Used Google Places API to verify geographic coordinates of the provided options.
