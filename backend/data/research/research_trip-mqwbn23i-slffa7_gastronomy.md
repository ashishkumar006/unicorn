# Goa Gastronomy Research Report

## Executive Summary
Based on the provided restaurant options and regional analysis for a budget of ₹10,000, the focus is centered on authentic Goan flavors. While the search results provided some out-of-state options (Delhi and MP), the primary recommendation for a trip *in* Goa is **Spice Goa Restaurant**, which offers the best value and authenticity within the budget constraints.

## Regional Culinary Insight
Goan cuisine is a unique blend of Konkani and Portuguese influences. To maximize a ₹10,000 budget, we recommend a mix of mid-range restaurants like Spice Goa and exploring local 'shacks' and street vendors.
