### Analysis & Findings

#### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Findings:** This option is highly cost-effective. The simulated booking for 1 guest confirms availability for the selected dates. It provides a high value-to-cost ratio, allowing the traveler to maintain the "semi-luxury" feel while staying well under the 10,000 INR cap.
- **Pros:** Extremely affordable, good ratings.
- **Cons:** Basic luxury compared to the others.

#### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Findings:** While this property offers a more refined luxury experience (Marriott Series), the total cost for 7 nights consumes nearly 91% of the total budget. This is only viable if the traveler has a separate budget for daily expenses.
- **Pros:** Superior amenities, professional service.
- **Cons:** High budget utilization.

#### 3. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Findings:** This is a premium 5-star experience. However, at ~2,100 INR per night, the total exceeds the 10,000 INR budget. It is excluded from the final recommendation based on financial constraints.
