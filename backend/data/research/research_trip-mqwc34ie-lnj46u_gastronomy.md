# 🍽️ Goa Gastronomy Research Report

## Executive Summary
Analysis of dining options for a budget of ₹10,000. Note: Initial search results contained out-of-state entries (Delhi, MP); these have been filtered out to focus exclusively on authentic Goan experiences. The focus is shifted toward **Spice Goa Restaurant** and local culinary hubs in Mapusa and Panjim to ensure budget efficiency and authenticity.

## 🏛️ Restaurant Analysis

### Spice Goa Restaurant
*   **Vibe:** Casual, family-friendly, highway-accessible.
*   **Cost Analysis:** Average plate cost is ₹500, making it highly sustainable for a ₹10k total trip budget. 
*   **Verdict:** Excellent for authentic regional flavors without the 'tourist tax' found in beach shacks.

## 🥘 Regional Culinary Specialties
Goan cuisine is a unique blend of Konkani and Portuguese influences. To stay within budget, a mix of high-rated local eateries and street food is recommended.

### Must-Try Dishes
1. **Fish Thali:** The gold standard of Goan lunches. Includes fish fry, curry, rice, and solkadhi.
2. **Bebinca:** A multi-layered Goan pudding.
3. **Xacuti:** A spicy, coconut-based curry.
