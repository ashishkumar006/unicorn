# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and schedules, the following platforms were simulated for search and data retrieval:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked flight fare finder for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOX/GOI) for passenger count: 1. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Simulated cab and bus search forms for intercity transit from Mumbai to Goa. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Checked Volvo Multi-Axle sleeper bus fares for the specified dates. |

## Comparison Analysis

### 1. Flight (Premium/Fastest)
Flights offer the fastest transit time. While the budget is 10,000 INR, a round trip on Indigo typically ranges from 7,000 to 9,000 INR if booked in advance. This leaves minimal room for local transport but fits within the global budget.

### 2. Train (Balanced/Scenic)
The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC or 2AC classes provide a balance of comfort and cost. It is the most cost-effective high-comfort option, keeping the total spend well under 5,000 INR.

### 3. Road/Bus (Budget/Flexible)
Private luxury buses (Volvo) are the most flexible. While longer than flights and slightly less comfortable than trains, they offer multiple drop-off points in North and South Goa.

## Final Findings
- **Best for Speed:** Indigo Flight
- **Best for Experience:** IRCTC Train (Konkan Route)
- **Best for Budget:** RedBus Luxury Coach
