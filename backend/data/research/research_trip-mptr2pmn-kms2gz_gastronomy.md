## Local Culinary Specialties
- **Fish Thali**: A staple consisting of rice, fish curry, fried fish, and vegetables.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered Goan pudding.
- **Xacuti**: A rich, spicy curry made with coconut and roasted spices.
