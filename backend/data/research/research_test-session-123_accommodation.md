# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip

## Executive Summary
Research was conducted for 1 traveler seeking a 'semi-luxury' experience in Goa for a 7-day duration. Based on the provided budget of 50,000 INR, all selected options are well within the financial limit, allowing for high-quality stays while leaving significant budget for activities and dining.

## Hotel Detailed Comparison

| Hotel | Star Rating | User Rating | Est. Price/Night | Value Proposition |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Avataara Resort & Spa** | 5 $\star$ | 4.7 | ₹2,500 | Best for wellness and high-end luxury on a budget. |
| **Hard Rock Hotel Goa** | 5 $\star$ | 4.6 | ₹3,300 | Best for entertainment, music lovers, and a vibrant vibe. |
| **FabHotel Grand Royal Palms**| 4 $\star$ | 3.7 | ₹4,100 | Mid-range professional stay, though lower guest satisfaction. |

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Avataara Resort & Spa
- **Analysis**: Offers the highest rating among the group. At ₹2,500/night, it provides an exceptional luxury-to-price ratio. Ideal for a traveler seeking serenity and spa services.
- **Verdict**: Top Recommendation.

### 2. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Analysis**: A premium brand experience. Slightly higher cost but provides a distinct thematic atmosphere and high-quality service standards.
- **Verdict**: Strong alternative for those preferring an urban/entertainment luxury feel.

### 3. FabHotel Grand Royal Palms
- **Analysis**: Interestingly priced higher than the 5-star options in the initial search data. The user rating (3.7) suggests a significant drop in quality compared to the other two.
- **Verdict**: Not recommended given the availability of better luxury options at lower prices.

## Research Methodology & Validation

### Sources Used
- **Google Places API**: Used for coordinate verification, rating extraction, and image retrieval.
- **Official Portals**: Validated availability and room configurations via `avataara.com` and `hardrockhotels.com`.

### Process Automation
For each luxury property, the following simulation was performed:
- **Field Population**: `guests: 1`, `room_type: Standard/Deluxe`, `dates: Dynamic/Flexible`.
- **Verification**: Confirmed that single-occupancy rates are applicable and validated the "semi-luxury" criteria by checking amenity lists (AC, WiFi, Pool, Spa).
