# Goa Tourism Comprehensive Guide

## 🏖️ Beaches
- **Calangute & Baga**: The most popular hubs for water sports and nightlife.
- **Palolem**: A crescent-shaped paradise ideal for kayaking and relaxation.
- **Colva**: Famous for its white sands and local food stalls.

## 🏛️ History & Architecture
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus**: A UNESCO World Heritage site containing the remains of St. Francis Xavier.
- **Aguada Fort**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort offering panoramic views of the Arabian Sea.
- **Chapora Fort**: The 'Dil Chahta Hai' fort, perfect for sunsets.

## 🌿 Nature & Waterfalls
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A massive four-tiered waterfall cascading down the Western Ghats.
- **Netravali Wildlife Sanctuary**: Pristine forests and biodiversity hotspots.

## 💡 Travel Tips
- **Transport**: Renting a scooty or car is the most efficient way to explore.
- **Timing**: Visit between November and February for ideal weather.
- **Dress Code**: Respectful attire is required when visiting churches and temples.
