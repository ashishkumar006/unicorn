# 🍽️ Goa Gastronomy Research Report

## Executive Summary
Based on the budget of ₹10,000, the primary focus is on authentic Goan flavors. Note: Initial search results contained several 'Goa Restaurants' located outside of Goa (Delhi, MP). I have filtered these out to focus exclusively on actual Goan establishments and regional specialties to ensure travel budget efficiency.

## 🔍 Restaurant Analysis

### Spice Goa Restaurant
*   **Vibe:** Casual/Family-oriented
*   **Cost Profile:** Budget-friendly (Avg ₹500/plate)
*   **Verdict:** Highly recommended for those seeking a local atmosphere and authentic coastal flavors without breaking the bank.

## 🥘 Local Culinary Map

### Regional Specialties
- **Goan Fish Curry:** The cornerstone of the region, best paired with steamed rice.
- **Xacuti:** A complex, spicy coconut-based curry.
- **Bebinca:** The iconic multi-layered Goan cake.
