# Mumbai to Goa Transit Report

## ✈️ Option 1: Flight (Fastest)
- **Airline:** IndiGo
- **Route:** BOM $\rightarrow$ GOI
- **Departure:** 07:30 AM | **Arrival:** 08:45 AM
- **Duration:** 1h 15m
- **Cost:** ₹4,200
- **Verdict:** Best for travelers maximizing their vacation time.

## 🚂 Option 2: Train (Balanced)
- **Train:** Konkan Railway / Tejas Express
- **Route:** CSMT $\rightarrow$ Madgaon
- **Departure:** 11:30 AM | **Arrival:** 11:00 PM
- **Duration:** 11h 30m
- **Cost:** ₹1,800 (3AC)
- **Verdict:** Most scenic route through the Western Ghats.

## 🚗 Option 3: Intercity Cab/Bus (Flexible)
- **Provider:** Private Operator/Volvo
- **Route:** Mumbai $\rightarrow$ Panjim
- **Departure:** 10:00 PM | **Arrival:** 07:00 AM
- **Duration:** 9h 00m
- **Cost:** ₹3,500 (Shared/Semi-Private)
- **Verdict:** Best for overnight travel to save on hotel costs.
