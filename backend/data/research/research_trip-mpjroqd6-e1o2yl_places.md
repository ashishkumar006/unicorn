# Research Report: Top Attractions in Goa

## 🏛️ Attraction Analysis

### Nature & Scenic
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A majestic four-tiered waterfall. Access usually involves a jeep safari through the Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary. High demand during monsoon seasons.

### History & Architecture
- **Fort Aguada**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort and lighthouse. Key strategic point for observing the Arabian Sea.
- **Cabo de Rama Fort**: An ancient cliffside fort offering panoramic views of the ocean and deep historical roots dating back to the Ptolemaic era.
