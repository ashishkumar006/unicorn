### Detailed Findings

#### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Analysis:** Extremely budget-friendly option. While it allows for significant savings, it may lean more towards 'budget' than 'semi-luxury'. 
- **Verdict:** Best for travelers wanting to save money for experiences/dining.

#### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa Goa
- **Analysis:** Strikes the best balance for a "semi-luxury" request. It fits within the 10,000 INR total budget while offering upscale amenities and a professional brand experience (Marriott series).
- **Verdict:** Recommended choice for this specific budget and style.

#### 3. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Analysis:** Highest luxury rating and amenities, but the cost for 7 nights (approx. 14,700 INR) significantly exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR.
- **Verdict:** Not feasible unless the budget is increased.
