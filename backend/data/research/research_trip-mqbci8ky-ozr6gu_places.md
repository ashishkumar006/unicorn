# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Overview
This report provides a detailed analysis of key tourist destinations in Goa, ranging from historical fortifications to natural wonders and serene coastlines. Each site has been evaluated for accessibility, time requirements, and visitor experience.

## Research Findings & Simulation

### 1. Dudhsagar Falls
- **Ticketing Simulation:** Simulated access via the Goa Forest Department portal. Entry typically involves a jeep safari fee and a forest entry permit. 
- **Occupancy Analysis:** High peak-hour congestion between 10 AM and 2 PM. Recommended early arrival to avoid queue delays.

### 2. Fort Aguada
- **Ticketing Simulation:** Checked local ASI (Archaeological Survey of India) guidelines. Entry is generally low-cost or free for certain sections, though guided tours are available.
- **Occupancy Analysis:** Steady flow of tourists; crowd peaks during sunset hours.

### 3. Velsao Beach
- **Ticketing Simulation:** Confirmed as a public beach with no official ticketing structure.
- **Occupancy Analysis:** Low density compared to Calangute/Baga, making it ideal for those seeking solitude.
