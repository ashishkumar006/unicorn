# 🌴 Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Region

## 🍽️ Culinary Overview
Goa offers a vibrant blend of Portuguese influence and traditional Indian coastal flavors. While the provided dataset contains some outliers located outside the state, the focus remains on the authentic Goan dining experience characterized by coconut, tamarind, and fresh seafood.

### 🍤 Local Specialties
- **Fish Curry Rice**: The staple meal of every Goan household.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional Goan cake.
- **Vindaloo**: A spicy, tangy pork dish with Portuguese roots.

### 📍 Food Alleys & Street Eats
For an authentic experience, explore the **Mapusa Market** and **Panjim Latin Quarter (Fontainhas)** for street-side snacks and fresh coconut water.

### 💰 Budget Analysis
- **Budget Meal**: ₹200 - ₹500
- **Mid-Range Experience**: ₹500 - ₹900
- **Fine Dining**: ₹1,500+

### 📅 Booking Recommendations
Most local establishments like Spice Goa operate on a walk-in basis. For high-end beach shacks during peak season (November to February), advance reservations are highly recommended.
