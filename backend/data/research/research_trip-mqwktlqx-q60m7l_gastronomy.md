## Detailed Analysis: Spice Goa Restaurant
Located near the Karaswada junction, this establishment serves as an authentic gateway to North Goan flavors. The pricing is well within the 10,000 INR trip budget, allowing for multiple high-quality meals without financial strain.

- **Strengths**: High rating for authenticity, strategic location for travelers moving between Mapusa and Calangute.
- **Cost Analysis**: With an average plate cost of ₹500, a couple can dine comfortably for under ₹1,200 per meal.

## Regional Culinary Recommendations
To maximize the 10,000 INR budget, I recommend a mix of sit-down dining and exploring the Panjim and Mapusa markets for local specialties.

### Must-Try Local Dishes
- **Fish Curry Rice**: The staple Goan meal.
- **Pork Vindaloo**: A spicy, tangy vinegar-based curry.
- **Bebinca**: A traditional layered dessert.
