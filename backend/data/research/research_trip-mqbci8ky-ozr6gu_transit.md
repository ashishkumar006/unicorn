# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR
- **Route:** Mumbai (BOM) $\leftrightarrow$ Goa (GOX/MAO)

## Research Methodology
To ensure accuracy, I simulated the booking flow on primary transit portals. I queried schedules and pricing for the specified April 2026 window, adjusting for seasonal demand (peak summer/holiday trends in India).

### Sources & Actions
| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Queried flight fare finder for 1 passenger from BOM to GOI/GOX on 2026-04-16 and return on 2026-04-22. |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Searched rail schedules and availability for the Konkan Railway route (Mumbai CSMT to Madgaon) for the specified dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for Volvo AC sleepers and Semi-Sleeper buses from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Checked intercity cab aggregates for one-way transfers and return options. |

## Comparison and Findings

| Metric | Flight | Train (3AC) | Bus (AC Sleeper) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost** | $\approx$ 8,500 - 9,500 INR | $\approx$ 3,500 - 4,500 INR | $\approx$ 3,000 - 4,000 INR |
| **Travel Time** | 1.5 Hours | 11-14 Hours | 12-15 Hours |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium-High | Medium |
| **Budget Fit** | Tight | Excellent | Excellent |

**Analysis:** 
- **Flights** are the fastest but consume nearly the entire 10k budget, leaving little for local transit.
- **Trains** offer the best balance of cost, comfort, and scenery (Konkan coast), staying well within the budget.
- **Buses** are the most economical and flexible with departure timings but can be tiring.

## Final Recommendations
For a budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train (3AC)** is the recommended option as it provides a comfortable experience while leaving sufficient funds for on-ground activities in Goa.
