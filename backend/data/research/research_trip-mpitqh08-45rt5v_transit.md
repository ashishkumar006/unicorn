# Transit Options: Mumbai to Goa

### ✈️ Option 1: Flight (Fastest)
- **Carrier:** IndiGo / Air India Express
- **Route:** BOM $\rightarrow$ GOI/GOX
- **Duration:** 1h 15m
- **Estimated Cost:** ₹4,500 - ₹6,500
- **Best for:** Travelers prioritizing time and convenience.

### 🚂 Option 2: Train (Scenic & Budget)
- **Train:** Tejas Express / Konkan Kanya
- **Route:** CSMT/Dadkar $\rightarrow$ Madgaon
- **Duration:** 12h - 15h
- **Estimated Cost:** ₹1,200 - ₹2,800
- **Best for:** Scenic views of the Western Ghats and budget management.

### 🚕 Option 3: Road / Intercity Cab (Flexible)
- **Route:** NH 48 / NH 66
- **Duration:** 10h - 12h
- **Estimated Cost:** ₹8,000 - ₹10,000 (Private) or ₹2,500 (Bus)
- **Best for:** Groups or those wanting to stop at Lonavala/Kolhapur along the way.
