### Detailed Breakdown: Spice Goa Restaurant
- **Culinary Profile**: Specializes in traditional Goan spices and coastal Indian preparations.
- **Budget Impact**: At ₹500/plate, a couple could dine here multiple times while staying well within the ₹10,000 total trip budget.
- **Accessibility**: Located on the Mumbai-Goa Highway, making it an ideal stop for travelers.

## 🍤 Local Culinary Specialties
Beyond formal dining, the following regional dishes are essential for a complete Goan experience:
- **Fish Curry Rice**: The staple diet of Goa; a balance of coconut and tamarind.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional Goan cake.
- **Ros Omelette**: A spicy street-side favorite.
