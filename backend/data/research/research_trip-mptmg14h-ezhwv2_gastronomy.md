## Local Culinary Recommendations
To maximize the ₹10,000 budget, I recommend a mix of sit-down restaurants and street-side shacks.

### Must-Try Regional Specialties
- **Goan Fish Curry**: A staple using coconut and tamarind.
- **Bebinca**: The traditional layered Goan cake.
- **Xacuti**: A complex, spicy curry made with roasted coconut.

## Budget Optimization Strategy
- **Budget**: ₹10,000
- **Estimated Daily Spend**: ₹1,000 - ₹1,500 (Food & Drinks)
- **Strategy**: Balance one 'Fine' meal at Spice Goa with street food explorations in Panjim and Mapusa markets.
