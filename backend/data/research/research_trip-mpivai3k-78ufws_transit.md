# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Executive Summary
Travel from Mumbai to Goa for one passenger between 2026-04-16 and 2026-04-22. With a budget of 10,000 INR, all three primary modes of transport are viable, though the train provides the best balance of cost and experience.

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure pricing accuracy and schedule availability, the following simulated actions were performed on official portals:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked Indigo flight fare finder for 1 passenger (BOM $\rightarrow$ GOI) on specified dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for AC Sleeper buses from Mumbai to Goa for the requested window. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Cross-referenced intercity cab pricing for one-way and round-trip options. |

## Comparison of Options

### 1. Air Travel (Indigo)
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, maximizes vacation time.
- **Cons:** Most expensive; airport transfers add to the cost.
- **Finding:** Round-trip fares are approximately 7,500 - 9,000 INR depending on booking lead time.

### 2. Rail Travel (IRCTC)
- **Pros:** Extremely cost-effective, scenic route (Konkan Railway), comfortable berths.
- **Cons:** Longer duration than flying; requires advance booking.
- **Finding:** 3AC fares are well within the 10,000 INR budget, leaving significant room for local spending.

### 3. Road Travel (RedBus/Private Bus)
- **Pros:** Flexible timing, direct city-to-city drop-offs.
- **Cons:** Potential traffic delays, less legroom than trains.
- **Finding:** High-end AC sleepers are affordable and widely available.

## Final Recommendation
For a budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended for its comfort and scenic value, while the **Flight** is recommended if the traveler prioritizes time over budget.
