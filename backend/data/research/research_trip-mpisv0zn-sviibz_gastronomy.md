# 🌴 Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Region

## 🍽️ Culinary Landscape
Goa's food scene is a vibrant fusion of traditional Portuguese influence and coastal Indian flavors. While the provided dataset includes some outlets outside the state, the core focus remains on the authentic Goan palate characterized by coconut, vinegar, and fresh seafood.

## 🍛 Local Specialties
- **Goan Fish Curry**: A coconut-based curry with a tangy edge from tamarind.
- **Pork Vindaloo**: A spicy, vinegar-infused pork dish reflecting Portuguese heritage.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional Goan dessert made of coconut milk and sugar.

## 🚶 Food Alleys & Street Eats
- **Mapusa Market**: Ideal for finding local snacks and authentic Goan sweets.
- **Panjim Latin Quarter**: Perfect for seafood appetizers and artisanal cakes.

## 📅 Booking & Tips
- **Booking**: Generally not required for casual spots like Spice Goa, but recommended for specialty themed restaurants during weekends.
- **Budgeting**: Average meal costs range from **₹500 to ₹900** per person.
- **Recommendation**: Focus on the Mapusa area for the most authentic regional experience.
