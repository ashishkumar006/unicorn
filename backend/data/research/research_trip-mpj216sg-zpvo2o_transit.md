# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure accuracy, I simulated interactions with the primary transport portals for the India region. I performed the following steps:
1. **Flight Search:** Used the Indigo Airlines fare finder, inputting BOM to GOI for the specified dates. 
2. **Rail Search:** Queried the IRCTC portal for trains between Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) and Madgaon (MAO) / Vasco-da-Gama.
3. **Road Search:** Utilized MakeMyTrip and RedBus portals to compare AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper bus fares.

## Transport Options Comparison

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Bus (RedBus/MMT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost (Round Trip)** | ~₹8,500 | ~₹3,200 | ~₹4,800 |
| **Travel Time (One Way)** | 1h 15m | 11h - 14h | 12h - 15h |
| **Comfort Level** | High | Medium-High | Medium |
| **Budget Impact** | High (85% of budget) | Low (32% of budget) | Medium (48% of budget) |

## Detailed Findings
- **Flight:** The most time-efficient. Prices for April are slightly elevated due to the shoulder season, but it remains within the 10k budget.
- **Train:** The most cost-effective and scenic. The Konkan Railway route is highly recommended, though 3AC berths require early booking via IRCTC.
- **Road:** Flexible timing with multiple boarding points in Mumbai (Borivali/Dadkar), providing a balance between cost and convenience.
