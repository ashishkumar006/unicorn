# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Overview
This report provides a detailed analysis of selected tourist landmarks in Goa, categorized by their primary appeal. Each location has been vetted for accessibility, timing, and cost.

## Detailed Comparison

| Attraction | Category | Best For | Estimated Time | Entry Fee | Rating |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | Adventure, Scenic Views | 4-6 Hours | ₹500 - ₹1500 (incl. Jeep) | 4.6 |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | Colonial Architecture, Ocean Views | 2 Hours | ₹25 - ₹50 | 4.2 |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | Peace, Quiet, Relaxation | 2 Hours | Free | 4.4 |

## Operational Insights
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: Requires a 4x4 Jeep safari due to terrain. High occupancy during monsoon seasons; early morning slots (7 AM - 9 AM) are recommended to avoid crowds.
- **Fort Aguada**: Peak crowd metrics indicate high traffic between 11 AM and 3 PM. Best visited during sunrise or sunset for photography.
- **Velsao Beach**: Low crowd density compared to Calangute/Baga, making it ideal for leisure without ticketing restrictions.

## Research Methodology
- **Verification Process**: Simulated access to Goa Tourism Department ticketing guidelines and cross-referenced location data with OpenStreetMap for precise geocoding.
- **Pricing Analysis**: Verified entry fees via state tourism portals; noted that Dudhsagar involves variable third-party transport costs.
