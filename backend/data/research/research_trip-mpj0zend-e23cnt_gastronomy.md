# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Dining Exploration

## Executive Summary
Based on the budget of ₹10,000, the focus is on authentic Goan culinary experiences. While several options were analyzed, only one aligns with the geographical location of Goa. The research emphasizes a mix of traditional Goan fish curries, pork vindaloo, and street-side delicacies to ensure a high-value gastronomic experience.

## Restaurant Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant
- **Verdict**: Highly Recommended. This is the only viable option located within Goa (Mapusa). 
- **Cost Analysis**: With an average cost of ₹500 per person, it fits perfectly within the budget, allowing for multiple visits or a lavish multi-course meal.
- **Experience**: Known for regional authenticity and accessibility.

*Note: Other identified 'Goa Restaurants' were excluded as they are located in New Delhi and Madhya Pradesh.*

## Regional Culinary Roadmap

### Must-Try Local Specialties
- **Goan Fish Curry**: The cornerstone of Goan cuisine. Best enjoyed with steamed basmati rice.
- **Pork Vindaloo**: A spicy, tangy dish influenced by Portuguese flavors.
- **Bebinca**: A layered Goan cake that serves as the perfect dessert.

## Budget Allocation Strategy
- **Fine Dining/Sit-down**: ₹3,000 - ₹4,000
- **Street Food & Cafes**: ₹2,000 - ₹3,000
- **Local Markets/Snacks**: ₹1,000 - ₹2,000
- **Buffer**: ₹1,000

## Research Methodology
- **Data Validation**: Cross-referenced addresses to ensure establishments were physically located in Goa.
- **Price Simulation**: Scanned digital menus to estimate plate costs based on current inflation and regional pricing.
- **Verification**: Validated ratings via Zomato and Google Places to ensure consistency in food quality.
