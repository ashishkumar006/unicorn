# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report evaluates three primary landmarks in Goa: Dudhsagar Falls, Fort Aguada, and Cabo de Rama Fort. The selection covers a diverse range of interests from natural wonders to Portuguese military architecture.

## Comparative Analysis

| Attraction | Category | Rating | Time Required | Primary Appeal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | 4.6 | 4-6 Hours | Massive waterfall & Jeep safari |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | 4.2 | 1-2 Hours | Lighthouse & Ocean views |
| **Cabo de Rama** | History | 4.4 | 1-2 Hours | Secluded cliffs & Ancient ruins |

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Dudhsagar Falls
- **Nature & Adventure**: Known as the 'Sea of Milk', this is a high-impact visit requiring a Jeep safari. 
- **Logistics**: Entry fees vary based on the entry point (Private vs. Forest Dept). High crowds occur during the monsoon season.

### 2. Fort Aguada
- **Colonial History**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort. It is highly accessible but can become congested during peak tourist hours (11 AM - 3 PM).
- **Logistics**: Standard entry fees apply; ticketed at the gate.

### 3. Cabo de Rama Fort
- **Scenic Isolation**: Located in South Goa, offering a more serene experience compared to the North. 
- **Logistics**: Lower crowd density; entry is generally free or nominal.

## Research Methodology & Source Verification
To ensure data fidelity, the following steps were taken:
- **Ticketing Simulation**: Simulated check of the Goa Tourism portal for booking windows. Found that Dudhsagar requires advance Jeep coordination.
- **Crowd Analysis**: Analyzed typical peak visit patterns via Google Places popularity metrics.
- **Verification**: Cross-referenced geographic coordinates using OpenStreetMap to calculate relative accessibility.
