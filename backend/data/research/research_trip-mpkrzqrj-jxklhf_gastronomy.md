# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Culinary Exploration

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes dining options for a trip to Goa with a total budget of ₹10,000. Based on the provided data, only **Spice Goa Restaurant** is geographically relevant to the destination. The other listed options are located in New Delhi and Madhya Pradesh and have been excluded from the final recommendations to ensure budget and logistical accuracy.

## Analysis of Dining Options

Located near Mapusa, this establishment serves as a high-value anchor for the trip. With an average cost of ₹500 per person, it allows for multiple high-quality meals while leaving significant room in the ₹10,000 budget for street food and local experiences.
