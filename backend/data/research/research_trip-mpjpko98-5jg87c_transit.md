# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Outbound Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Source Verification & Actions
To ensure accuracy, the following actions were performed on official booking portals:
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal:** Queried the rail schedules search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Goa (Madgaon/Thivim) for 2026-04-16 and 2026-04-22. Verified 3AC and Sleeper class availability.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking:** Accessed the flight fare finder for 1 adult passenger on the specified dates. Simulated the booking flow from BOM to GOI/GOX.
- **RedBus Portal:** Filled out the intercity bus search form to compare luxury AC sleeper buses and semi-sleeper options.
- **MakeMyTrip Portal:** Checked the cab rental and one-way drop search forms for private vehicle pricing.

## Comparison Analysis

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (RedBus/MMT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost** | ~₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ~₹3,200 - ₹4,500 | ~₹4,000 - ₹6,000 |
| **Travel Time** | 1h 15m | 12h - 15h | 14h - 18h |
| **Comfort** | High (Fastest) | Medium (Sleeper/3AC) | Medium (AC Sleeper) |
| **Budget Fit** | Tight/Over | Excellent | Excellent |

## Final Findings
- **Flight:** While the fastest, flights on these dates are pushing the budget limit (~₹4,200 each way). 
- **Train:** The most cost-effective and reliable option, specifically the Konkan Railway route, which offers scenic views.
- **Road:** AC Sleeper buses provide a good balance of cost and flexibility, though travel times are longer than rail.
