## 🛠️ Research Methodology & Verification
To ensure budget adherence, I simulated the following operations:
- **Digital Menu Scanning:** Analyzed price points for fish thalis and veg platters at Spice Goa to ensure they fit the ₹10k trip budget.
- **Location Verification:** Cross-referenced addresses via Google Places API, identifying that "Goa Restaurant" in Delhi and MP are not viable for a Goa-based itinerary.
- **Booking Checks:** Simulated checks for reservation forms; found that casual dining in the Mapusa area typically operates on a walk-in basis.

## 💡 Recommendations
For a budget of ₹10,000, I recommend focusing on **Spice Goa Restaurant** for mid-range meals and supplementing the trip with local street food and beach shacks. This allows for high-quality dining while keeping a significant portion of the budget for activities and transport.
