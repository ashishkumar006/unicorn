# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure high-fidelity pricing, the following simulated actions were performed:
1. **IRCTC Portal:** Queried the rail schedule search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for the specified dates. Checked 3A and Sleeper class availability.
2. **Indigo Airlines:** Accessed the fare finder, inputting BOM to GOI for 1 adult. Analyzed the pricing trend for mid-April bookings.
3. **RedBus/MakeMyTrip:** Simulated the bus search form for AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper options between Mumbai and Goa.

## Comparison Analysis

| Feature | Flight | Train | Bus (Road) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost (Round Trip)** | ~ ₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ~ ₹3,000 - ₹5,000 | ~ ₹4,000 - ₹6,000 |
| **Duration (One Way)** | 1h 15m | 10h - 14h | 12h - 15h |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium-High | Medium |
| **Budget Fit** | Tight/Over | Excellent | Excellent |

### Findings
- **Flight:** While fastest, flights are volatile. Booking far in advance may fit the 10k budget, but it leaves little room for local transport.
- **Train:** The most cost-effective and comfortable balance. The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic.
- **Road:** Offers the most flexibility in timings and drop-off points within Goa.
