# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Dining Analysis

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes dining options for a traveler with a total budget of ₹10,000. Upon reviewing the provided restaurant data, a critical geographic discrepancy was identified: only **Spice Goa Restaurant** is actually located in Goa. The other listed 'Goa Restaurants' are located in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh and have been excluded from the recommendation set to ensure trip accuracy.

## Comparative Analysis

### Top Recommendation: Spice Goa Restaurant
Located near Mapusa, this establishment is the only viable option within the target region. It offers a balanced price point that fits well within the ₹10,000 overall trip budget, allowing for multiple high-quality meals without financial strain.

## Local Culinary Guide
To maximize the ₹10,000 budget, the user should blend restaurant visits with authentic street food and local shacks.

### Must-Try Regional Specialties
1. **Fish Curry Rice**: The staple of Goan households.
2. **Pork Vindaloo**: A spicy, tangy vinegar-based curry.
3. **Bebinca**: A multi-layered Goan dessert.
