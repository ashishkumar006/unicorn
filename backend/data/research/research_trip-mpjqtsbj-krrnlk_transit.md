## Journey Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 (Departure) to 2026-04-22 (Return)
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR
- **Route:** Mumbai (BOM) $\leftrightarrow$ Goa (GOI/GOX)

## Detailed Comparison

### 1. Air Travel (Indigo)
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, reduces travel fatigue.
- **Cons:** Most expensive; consumes a large portion of the budget.
- **Finding:** Round-trip fares hover around 7,000 - 9,000 INR if booked early.

### 2. Rail Travel (IRCTC)
- **Pros:** Most cost-effective, scenic Konkan route, comfortable overnight travel.
- **Cons:** Subject to availability (Waitlist risks).
- **Finding:** 3AC fares are highly budget-friendly, fitting well within the 10k limit.

### 3. Road Travel (RedBus/MMT)
- **Pros:** Flexible timings, multiple drop-off points.
- **Cons:** Longest duration, potential for road fatigue.
- **Finding:** AC Sleeper buses provide a middle-ground price point.

## Final Recommendation
For a balance of cost and experience, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended due to the scenic beauty of the Western Ghats. For speed, **Indigo** is viable while staying under budget.
