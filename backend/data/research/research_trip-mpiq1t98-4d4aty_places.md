# Goa Tourism Comprehensive Guide

## 🏖️ Beaches
- **Calangute & Baga**: The most popular hubs for water sports and nightlife.
- **Palolem**: A crescent-shaped paradise perfect for relaxation and kayaking.

## 🏛️ History & Architecture
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus**: A UNESCO World Heritage site housing the remains of St. Francis Xavier.
- **Aguada Fort**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort offering panoramic ocean views.

## 🌿 Nature & Waterfalls
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A spectacular four-tiered waterfall known as the 'Sea of Milk'.

### 💡 Pro Travel Tips
- **Transport**: Rent a scooter or car; it's the most efficient way to navigate the state.
- **Timing**: Visit between November and February for the best weather.
- **Dress Code**: Modest clothing is required when entering churches and temples.
