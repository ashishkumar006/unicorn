# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and scheduling, the following simulations were performed on official booking portals:

### Sources & Actions
| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return on 2026-04-22. Checked 3AC and Sleeper class availability. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Used the fare finder for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOX/GOI) for a single passenger. Simulated round-trip selection for mid-April dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Searched Volvo AC Sleeper bus operators from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon for the specified dates. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Cross-referenced intercity cab pricing for one-way and round-trip journeys. |

## Comparison Analysis

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (RedBus/Volvo) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Travel Time** | ~1h 15m | ~10h - 12h | ~12h - 15h |
| **Cost (Round Trip)** | ~₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ~₹2,800 - ₹5,500 | ~₹4,000 - ₹6,000 |
| **Comfort** | High (Fastest) | Moderate (Scenic) | Moderate (Flexible) |
| **Budget Fit** | Tight/Over | Excellent | Excellent |

### Findings
1. **Flight:** While the fastest, flights for April (peak season transition) can fluctuate. To stay within the 10k budget, early booking is critical. 
2. **Train:** The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic and the most cost-effective option. 3AC is recommended for a balance of comfort and cost.
3. **Road:** Volvo sleeper buses provide a reliable alternative if train tickets are waitlisted, fitting comfortably within the budget.
