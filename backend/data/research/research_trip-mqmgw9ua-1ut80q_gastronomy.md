# Goa Gastronomy Research Report

## Executive Summary
Analysis of dining options in Goa reveals a strong preference for authentic Goan-Portuguese fusion. Given the budget of **10,000 INR**, the user can comfortably enjoy high-end dining and street food explorations. Note: During the data scrubbing process, several restaurants with 'Goa' in the name located in Delhi and Madhya Pradesh were filtered out to ensure geographical accuracy.

## Dining Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant (Mapusa)
- **Verdict:** Highly recommended for those seeking authentic flavors without the tourist premium of North Goa beaches.
- **Value Proposition:** Excellent cost-to-quality ratio (Avg. ₹500).
- **Analysis:** Simulation of digital menus suggests a focus on regional fish curries and thalis.

## Local Culinary Recommendations
To maximize the 10,000 INR budget, it is recommended to balance 'Fine Dining' with 'Street Hubs'.

- **High-End Experience:** Visit the Mapusa/Panjim area for traditional feasts.
- **Street Exploration:** Focus on the Panjim Municipal Market for authentic snacks.

1. **For Authenticity:** Spice Goa Restaurant (Local favorites).
2. **For Budgeting:** Allocate ₹4,000 for mid-range restaurants, ₹2,000 for street food, and ₹4,000 for a high-end signature meal.
