# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (7 nights)
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR (Total)
- **Luxury Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Hotel Analysis & Comparison

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Verdict:** Highly Recommended (Budget Friendly)
- **Analysis:** With a rate of ~500 INR per night, this is the only option that comfortably fits within the total 10,000 INR budget while leaving room for food and activities. It provides a solid 4-star experience at an entry-level price point.
- **Price for 7 nights:** ~3,500 INR

### 2. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Verdict:** Budget Stretch
- **Analysis:** At 1,300 INR per night, the total cost for 7 nights would be 9,100 INR. While this fits the absolute budget, it leaves only 900 INR for the entire week's dining and transport, which is unrealistic for a 'semi-luxury' experience.
- **Price for 7 nights:** ~9,100 INR

### 3. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Verdict:** Over Budget
- **Analysis:** At 2,100 INR per night, the total cost exceeds the trip budget (14,700 INR). This option is discarded based on the strict 10,000 INR total trip constraint.
- **Price for 7 nights:** ~14,700 INR
