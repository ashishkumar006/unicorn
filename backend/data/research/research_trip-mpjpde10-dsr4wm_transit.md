# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Methodology & Sources
To ensure the most accurate and cost-optimized options, the following official portals were simulated:
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal**: Queried for train availability between CSMT/LTT and Madgaon (MAO)/Vasco-da-Gama. 
- **Indigo Airlines Booking**: Simulated the fare finder for BOM to GOI/GOX for a single passenger.
- **RedBus Portal**: Checked Volvo AC Multi-axle sleeper and seater schedules for Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon.

## Comparison of Transport Options

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (RedBus) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Est. Cost** | ~₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ~₹2,800 - ₹4,500 | ~₹3,500 - ₹5,000 |
| **Travel Time** | 1h 15m | 10h - 13h | 12h - 15h |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium | Medium |
| **Budget Alignment**| Tight/Over | Well Within | Well Within |

## Findings
1. **Flight**: While the fastest, round-trip fares for April (peak season) can fluctuate. It barely fits the budget if booked in advance.
2. **Train**: The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic and the most cost-effective for 3AC/2AC sleepers.
3. **Road**: High availability and flexibility with multiple drop points in North and South Goa.

## Final Recommendations
For the best balance of cost and experience, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended for the scenic Konkan coast. For maximum speed, the **Flight** is viable if booked early.
