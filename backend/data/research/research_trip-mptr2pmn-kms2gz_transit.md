# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure pricing accuracy, simulations were conducted across primary booking portals. Since 2026 dates are beyond the immediate 120-day booking window for Indian Railways and most airlines, pricing is based on historical seasonal averages for mid-April (Peak Summer/Spring) and current trend analysis.

### Sources & Actions
| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules and fare structures for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for April 16 and 22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Simulated flight fare finder search for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOX/GOI) for 1 passenger. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Searched for intercity cab estimates and luxury bus pricing for Mumbai to Goa route. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Checked AC Sleeper bus availability and pricing for the Mumbai-Goa corridor. |

## Comparative Analysis

| Mode | Est. Cost (Round Trip) | Duration (One Way) | Comfort | Recommendation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Flight** | ₹7,500 - ₹9,000 | 1h 15m | High | Best for time-saving. |
| **Train** | ₹2,400 - ₹3,500 | 12h - 15h | Medium | Best for budget/scenery. |
| **Road (Bus)** | ₹3,000 - ₹4,500 | 14h - 18h | Medium | Flexible timing. |

## Findings
1. **Flight:** While the fastest, flights consume nearly 90% of the total budget. Booking 3-4 months in advance is critical to stay under ₹10,000.
2. **Train:** The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC is the recommended balance of cost and comfort.
3. **Road:** Private cabs are prohibitively expensive for a single traveler (exceeding budget); therefore, luxury AC Sleeper buses are the optimized road choice.
