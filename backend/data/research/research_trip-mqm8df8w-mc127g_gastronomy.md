# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Culinary Exploration

## Executive Summary
Based on the provided data and budget constraints (10,000 INR), the research focuses on authentic Goan flavors. While the initial dataset contained outliers (restaurants in Delhi and MP), the analysis has been filtered to prioritize the legitimate Goa-based establishment to ensure budget efficiency and local authenticity.

## Restaurant Comparison & Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant (Mapusa)
*   **Analysis**: This is the primary recommended spot from the provided list. Located strategically on the Mumbai-Goa Highway, it offers a balance of traditional Goan seafood and multi-cuisine options. With an average cost of ₹500 per person, it fits perfectly within the total trip budget, allowing for multiple visits or group dining without financial strain.
*   **Vibe**: Casual, family-friendly, and accessible.
*   **Verdict**: **Highly Recommended** for those traversing the North Goa region.

## Local Culinary Recommendations
To maximize the 10,000 INR budget, travelers should supplement restaurant visits with local shack dining and street markets.

### Must-Try Local Specialties
- **Fish Thali**: A staple of Goan lunch culture featuring rice, fish curry, fried fish, and vegetables.
- **Bebinca**: The traditional layered Goan cake.
- **Xacuti**: A rich, spicy curry made with roasted coconut.
