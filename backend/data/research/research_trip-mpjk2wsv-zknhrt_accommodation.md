# Accommodation Research Report: Goa

## Overview
Research conducted for 1 traveler visiting Goa from **2026-04-16 to 2026-04-17**. The goal was to find a "semi-luxury" option within a total trip budget of 10,000 INR. 

## Comparison Analysis

| Hotel | Rating | Stars | Price/Night | Style | Value Proposition |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Shelsta Holiday Resort** | 4.3 | 4 | 1,750 INR | Budget-Luxury | Most economical; leaves significant budget for activities. |
| **The Fern Kadamba** | 4.3 | 4 | 2,550 INR | Mid-Range | Balanced luxury with Marriott standards in Panjim. |
| **Hard Rock Hotel Goa** | 4.6 | 5 | 3,350 INR | Premium | High-end experience, best rating, slightly higher cost. |

### Detailed Findings

1. **Hard Rock Hotel Goa**: This represents the peak of the "semi-luxury" request. With a 4.6 rating and 5-star service, it offers the most immersive experience. Even at 3,350 INR, it fits well within the 10,000 INR total trip budget.
2. **The Fern Kadamba**: An excellent middle-ground choice. Being part of the Marriott series, it ensures consistent quality and professionalism, located centrally in Panjim.
3. **Shelsta Holiday Resort**: Provides a comfortable 4-star stay at a very low price point, ideal if the traveler prefers to spend more on dining and excursions.
