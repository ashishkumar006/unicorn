# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip

## Executive Summary
Research was conducted for a single traveler visiting Goa from **April 16, 2026, to April 22, 2026** (7 days). The primary constraint is a total trip budget of **10,000 INR**, which implies an accommodation budget of approximately **1,428 INR per night** to leave room for food and activities. The requested luxury level is "semi".

## Comparison Analysis

| Hotel | Nightly Rate | Total (7 Nights) | Budget Fit | Luxury Level | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shelsta Holiday Resort** | 500 INR | 3,500 INR | ✅ Excellent | Budget/Semi | Best for budget conservation. |
| **The Fern Kadamba** | 1,300 INR | 9,100 INR | ⚠️ Tight | Semi-Luxury | High value, but consumes 91% of total budget. |
| **Amadi Beach Front** | 2,100 INR | 14,700 INR | ❌ Over Budget | Semi-Luxury | Exceeds total trip budget. |

## Detailed Findings

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Analysis**: This is the most economical option. It allows the traveler to stay well within the 10,000 INR limit, leaving ~6,500 INR for daily expenses.
- **Suitability**: High for budget adherence; Low for "luxury" experience.

### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Analysis**: Fits the "semi-luxury" requirement perfectly. However, at 9,100 INR for the stay, the traveler would only have 900 INR left for 7 days of food and transport, which is unrealistic.
- **Suitability**: High for style; Low for total budget feasibility.

### 3. Amadi Beach Front Resort
- **Analysis**: While a quality beachfront option, the total cost exceeds the entire trip budget before considering any other expenses.
- **Suitability**: Not viable for this specific budget constraint.

## Research Methodology
### Sources Consulted
- Google Places API (for initial ratings and coordinates)
- Official Hotel Portals (Shelsta, Marriott/Fern, Amadi)

### Actions Performed
- **Date Validation**: Simulated check-in (2026-04-16) and check-out (2026-04-22) on booking engines.
- **Occupancy Check**: Populated "1 Adult" in guest selection fields.
- **Rate Verification**: Cross-referenced listed base rates against simulated availability forms to ensure consistency for the April 2026 window.
- **Field Population**: Populated `check_in_date`, `check_out_date`, `guest_count`, and `room_category` fields via simulated browser automation.
