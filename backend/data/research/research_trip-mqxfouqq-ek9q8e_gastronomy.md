# Gastronomy Research Report: Goa Dining Analysis

## Executive Summary
Based on the provided dataset, only one establishment is actually located within the state of Goa (**Spice Goa Restaurant**). The other listed options are located in New Delhi and Madhya Pradesh and have been excluded from the final culinary recommendation to ensure geographic accuracy for the user's trip.

## Detailed Restaurant Analysis

### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant
- **Verdict**: Highly Recommended for authentic regional flavors and budget-friendly dining.
- **Analysis**: This establishment serves as a primary hub for those traveling along the Mumbai-Goa Highway. It offers a balance of traditional Goan seafood and veg options.
- **Budget Impact**: With an average cost of ₹500, it fits perfectly within a 10,000 INR total trip budget, allowing for multiple high-quality meals.

## Local Culinary Guide: Goa

### Regional Specialties to Seek
Beyond the restaurant list, travelers in Goa should prioritize:
- **Goan Fish Curry**: The staple of the region, typically made with coconut milk and tamarind.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional Goan dessert.
- **Xacuti**: A spicy, coconut-based curry usually made with chicken or lamb.
