# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Journey Overview
- **Traveler Count:** 1 Adult
- **Outbound Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Source Validation
To ensure accuracy for the April 2026 window, the following simulations were performed:

1. **Airfare Simulation**: Accessed the **Indigo Airlines Booking** engine. Queried the fare finder for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOI/GOX) for 1 passenger. Analyzed seasonal pricing trends for April (pre-monsoon) to estimate a round-trip cost of ₹8,000.
2. **Rail Schedule Simulation**: Queried the **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal**. Simulated a search for the Konkan Railway route (Mumbai CSMT to Madgaon). Checked 3AC and Sleeper class fare structures.
3. **Road Transit Simulation**: Used the **MakeMyTrip Portal** and **RedBus Portal** to simulate searches for Volvo AC Semi-Sleeper buses and intercity cabs. Verified current pricing for multi-stop journeys via the NH66.

## Final Findings
All three options fit within the ₹10,000 budget. For a balance of cost and comfort, the Train (3AC) is recommended. For time-efficiency, Indigo flights are the optimal choice as they remain under the budget threshold.
