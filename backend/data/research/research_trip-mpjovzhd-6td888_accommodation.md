# Accommodation Research Report: Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1 Adult
- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (6 nights)
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR (Total)
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Detailed Hotel Comparison

### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Status:** Recommended (Within Budget)
- **Analysis:** This is the only option that aligns with the total trip budget of 10,000 INR. With a nightly rate of approximately 500 INR, the total stay cost is ~3,000 INR, leaving ample room for dining and activities.
- **Pros:** Extremely cost-effective, 4-star rating, high value for money.
- **Cons:** Basic luxury compared to high-end chains.

### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa Goa
- **Status:** Over Budget
- **Analysis:** At 1,300 INR/night, the total cost for 6 nights is ~7,800 INR. While this fits the 10,000 INR limit, it leaves only 2,200 INR for the entire week of food and travel, which is unrealistic for a 'semi-luxury' experience.
- **Pros:** Marriott affiliation, spa facilities, Panjim location.
- **Cons:** Consumes nearly 80% of the total budget on lodging alone.

### 3. Hard Rock Hotel Goa
- **Status:** Over Budget
- **Analysis:** At 2,100 INR/night, the total stay exceeds 12,600 INR, which is already over the total trip budget before adding any other expenses.
- **Pros:** Premium 5-star experience, iconic brand, high ratings.
- **Cons:** Financially non-viable for this specific budget constraint.

## Final Findings
To maintain a 'semi-luxury' feel while staying within a strict 10,000 INR total budget for a week, **Shelsta Holiday Resort** is the only viable choice. It provides a 4-star environment without depleting the funds needed for the rest of the trip.
