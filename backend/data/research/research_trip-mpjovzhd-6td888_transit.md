# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Process & Sources
To ensure pricing accuracy for 2026, simulations were performed on official booking portals using seasonal average pricing for the April window.

### Sources Consulted
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal**: Queried for rail availability and fare structures for the Konkan Railway route.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking**: Checked fare-finder for Mumbai (BOM) to Goa (GOI/GOX) sectors.
- **RedBus Portal**: Simulated search for AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper bus options from Mumbai to Panjim/Calangute.
- **MakeMyTrip Portal**: Evaluated intercity cab pricing for one-way and round-trip shared options.

### Actions Performed
1. **Rail:** Queried IRCTC search form for the *Konkan Kanya* and *Tejas Express* schedules for 2026-04-16.
2. **Air:** Filled Indigo flight search form for 1 passenger, BOM $\rightarrow$ GOI, comparing morning vs. evening slots.
3. **Road:** Checked RedBus filters for 'Top Rated' operators and 'AC Sleeper' configurations for the specified dates.

## Comparative Analysis

| Feature | Flight | Train | Bus (Road) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Cost (Return)** | ~₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ~₹2,500 - ₹4,500 | ~₹3,000 - ₹5,000 |
| **Travel Time** | 1.5 Hours | 10-12 Hours | 12-14 Hours |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium-High | Medium |
| **Scenic Value** | Low | Extremely High | Medium |

### Findings
- **Flight:** The fastest option, but potentially exceeds the 10k budget if not booked 6+ months in advance. 
- **Train:** The most cost-effective and scenic route via the Western Ghats. Highly recommended for the budget.
- **Road:** Offers the most flexibility in drop-off points (e.g., Calangute, Baga) but is the most taxing on the body.

## Final Recommendations
For a budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train** is the optimal choice, leaving significant funds for accommodation and activities. The **Bus** is a viable backup for direct hotel drops.
