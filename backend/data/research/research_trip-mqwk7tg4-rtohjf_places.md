# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report evaluates three key attractions in Goa, spanning nature and historical heritage. The selection includes the iconic Dudhsagar Falls, the historic Fort Aguada, and the serene Kesarval Spring. 

## Detailed Analysis

### 1. Nature & Waterfalls
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A premier destination known for its dramatic four-tiered descent. High crowds are typical during peak monsoon/winter seasons. Access requires jeep rentals via the forest department.
- **Kesarval Spring**: A more tranquil, off-the-beaten-path location suitable for those seeking peace over popularity.

### 2. History & Heritage
- **Fort Aguada**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort providing strategic views of the Arabian Sea. It is a high-traffic area with a well-defined tourist circuit.
