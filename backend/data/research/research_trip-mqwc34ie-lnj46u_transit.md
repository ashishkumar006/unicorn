# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure cost-optimization and accuracy, the following official portals were simulated for data retrieval:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules and fares for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Goa (MAO/MAJL) for 2026-04-16 and return 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked flight fare finder for one-way and round-trip fares from BOM to GOX/GOI for the specified dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Searched for AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper bus fares between Mumbai and Goa for April 2026. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Simulated cab search for one-way and round-trip intercity transfers to compare costs against public transport. |

## Detailed Comparison and Findings

### 1. Air Travel (Indigo)
Flights offer the fastest transit but are the most expensive. While a round trip can fit within the 10,000 INR budget if booked well in advance (approx 7,000 - 9,000 INR), it leaves very little room for local transit. 

### 2. Rail Travel (IRCTC)
The train is the most balanced option. The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC or Sleeper classes comfortably fit the budget, leaving significant funds for other activities. 

### 3. Road Travel (RedBus/Bus)
Buses are the most flexible and cost-effective. AC Sleeper buses provide a decent balance of comfort and price, ensuring the traveler stays well under the 10,000 INR limit.

## Final Recommendation
For a balance of budget and experience, the **Train (IRCTC)** is recommended due to the scenic Konkan coast route and cost-efficiency. For speed, **Indigo** is the best choice.
