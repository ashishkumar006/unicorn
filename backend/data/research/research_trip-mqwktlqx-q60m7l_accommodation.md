# Accommodation Research Report: Goa

## Trip Overview

- **Dates:** 2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22 (7 Nights)

- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

## Detailed Research Findings

### 1. Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim, Goa
- **Analysis:** This is the only viable option within the user's strict budget. At an estimated 500-1,200 INR per night, it fits the budget while maintaining a 4-star standard. 
- **Availability Check:** Simulated booking for 1 adult. Rooms are available for the requested dates. 
- **Verdict:** Recommended for budget compliance.

### 2. Novotel Goa Dona Sylvia Resort
- **Analysis:** While a high-quality 5-star experience, the estimated rate of 1,300+ INR per night (and likely higher during peaks) would exceed the total budget of 10,000 INR when combined with other trip expenses, though it is technically close if only accommodation is considered. However, standard rates for 7 nights would likely be ~9,100+ INR, leaving almost no budget for food or activities.
- **Verdict:** High risk of budget overflow.

### 3. Avataara Resort & Spa
- **Analysis:** Pure luxury. At 2,100+ INR per night, the total for 7 nights would be ~14,700 INR, which significantly exceeds the total trip budget of 10,000 INR.
- **Verdict:** Excluded due to budget constraints.
