# Goa Gastronomy Research Report

## Executive Summary
Analysis of dining options within a ₹10,000 budget. Note: Initial dataset contained outliers (restaurants in Delhi and MP); research has been pivoted to focus exclusively on authentic Goan establishments in the Mapusa/North Goa region to ensure budget efficiency and culinary authenticity.

## Detailed Recommendations
### 1. Spice Goa Restaurant
- **Analysis**: Excellent value for money. Ideal for travelers on a strict budget who want a mix of traditional Goan seafood and North Indian staples.
- **Budget Impact**: Low. Multiple meals can be enjoyed here without breaching the 10k limit.

## Local Culinary Guide
### Regional Specialties
- **Fish Curry Rice**: The staple Goan meal.
- **Bebinca**: A multi-layered traditional Goan cake.
- **Xacuti**: A rich, spicy coconut-based curry.
