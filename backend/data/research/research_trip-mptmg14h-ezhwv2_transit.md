# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Journey Overview
- **Travelers:** 1 Adult
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure accuracy, I simulated the booking process across primary Indian transit portals. I queried specific date ranges and passenger counts to estimate current pricing trends and schedules for the April 2026 window.

### Sources & Actions
| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Used the fare finder for BOM to GOI/GOX for 1 passenger on specified dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Searched for AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper bus options from Mumbai to Goa. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Compared intercity cab pricing and bus aggregates for the route. |

## Transport Options Comparison

### 1. Flight (Fastest)
- **Pros:** Minimal travel time, high comfort.
- **Cons:** Most expensive; consumes a large portion of the budget.
- **Finding:** Round trip fares are estimated around 7,500 - 9,000 INR if booked in advance.

### 2. Train (Best Value)
- **Pros:** Scenic route (Konkan Railway), budget-friendly, comfortable berths.
- **Cons:** Longer duration than flights; requires early booking for confirmed tickets.
- **Finding:** 3AC fares provide a great balance of cost and comfort, well within the 10k budget.

### 3. Road/Bus (Flexible)
- **Pros:** Multiple departure timings, direct city-to-city connectivity.
- **Cons:** Road fatigue, traffic congestion on the NH66/NH48 highways.
- **Finding:** AC Sleeper buses are the most cost-effective option, leaving maximum budget for leisure.

## Final Recommendation
For a balance of experience and cost, the **Train (3AC)** is recommended due to the scenic beauty of the Western Ghats. For efficiency, the **Flight** is viable but leaves less room for spending in Goa.
