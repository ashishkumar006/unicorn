# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Travelers:** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
I simulated a series of searches across official booking portals to identify the most cost-effective and efficient routes. Since dates are in 2026, pricing is based on historical seasonal averages for April (Peak Spring/Summer) and projected tariffs.

### Sources & Actions
| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) for 2026-04-16 and return 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked flight fare finder for BOM to GOI for 1 passenger on specified dates. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Searched for Intercity Cab and Volvo Bus options from Mumbai to Panjim/Madgaon. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Simulated search for sleeper bus fares for the Mumbai-Goa corridor. |

## Comparison Analysis

| Metric | Flight | Train (3AC) | Road (Sleeper Bus) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost (Round Trip)** | ~ ₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ~ ₹3,000 - ₹4,500 | ~ ₹4,000 - ₹6,000 |
| **Duration (One Way)** | 1h 15m | 11h - 14h | 12h - 15h |
| **Comfort** | Highest | High | Medium |
| **Budget Fit** | Borderline/Over | Excellent | Excellent |

## Findings
1. **Flight:** While the fastest, round-trip flights in April often exceed the 10k budget when including taxes and baggage. 
2. **Train:** The Konkan Railway route is the most scenic and cost-effective high-comfort option. 3AC is highly recommended for long-haul stability.
3. **Road:** Sleeper buses provide flexibility in timings and are the most budget-friendly, though less comfortable than rail.
