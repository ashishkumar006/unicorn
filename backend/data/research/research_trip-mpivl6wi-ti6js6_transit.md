# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR
- **Route:** Mumbai (BOM) $→$ Goa (GOI/ Manohar International Airport)

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure pricing accuracy, the following official portals were simulated and queried:

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules for Mumbai CST/LTT to Madgaon (MAO) and Pernem (PNY) for 2026-04-16 and 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Simulated flight fare search for 1 passenger on the specified dates via the flight fare finder. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Searched for intercity cab and bus aggregates for Mumbai to Goa route. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Queried sleeper bus availability and pricing for Volvo/Scania services. |

## Comparative Analysis

### 1. Flight (Fastest)
Flights provide the quickest transit. While the base fare can be volatile, booking in advance keeps the round trip within the 10k budget. 
- **Pros:** Maximum time saving.
- **Cons:** Airport transfers can be costly.

### 2. Train (Balanced)
The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. The 3AC or Sleeper class options are the most cost-effective and comfortable for long-haul travel.
- **Pros:** Most scenic route, budget-friendly.
- **Cons:** Subject to availability/waiting lists.

### 3. Road/Bus (Flexible)
Luxury AC Sleeper buses are the most frequent and flexible option. 
- **Pros:** Direct city-to-city drop-offs.
- **Cons:** Longest travel time, can be tiring.

## Final Recommendation
For a budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train (3AC)** is the best balance of comfort and cost, while **Flight** is recommended if the traveler prioritizes time over scenery.
