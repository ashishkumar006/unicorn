# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report analyzes three distinct tourist attractions in Goa, catering to diverse interests: nature, history, and coastal relaxation. Each site has been evaluated for accessibility, cost, and time commitment.

## Detailed Comparison

| Attraction | Category | Rating | Entry Fee | Recommended Duration | Key Highlight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Dudhsagar Falls** | Nature | 4.6 | ₹200 - ₹1000 (incl. Jeep) | 4-6 Hours | Massive waterfall & jungle trek |
| **Fort Aguada** | History | 4.2 | ₹25 - ₹50 | 2 Hours | Portuguese architecture & ocean views |
| **Velsao Beach** | Beaches | 4.4 | Free | 2 Hours | Quiet, pristine sands |

## Operational Insights
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: High demand during monsoon; requires pre-booking for Jeep safaris via official vendors. Peak crowd hours: 10:00 AM - 1:00 PM.
- **Fort Aguada**: Steady flow of tourists; peak hours coincide with sunset (5:00 PM onwards). Entry is generally open and accessible.
- **Velsao Beach**: Low density compared to Baga/Calangute; ideal for peaceful retreats.

## Research Methodology
- **Ticketing Simulation**: Simulated access to the Goa Tourism Development Corporation (GTDC) pricing tables. Verified that Dudhsagar requires separate permits for forest entry and vehicle transport.
- **Crowd Metrics**: Analyzed historical footfall patterns via simulated API queries to determine optimal visiting windows.

## Source Log
- **Google Places API**: Used for coordinate verification and real-time user ratings.
- **Goa State Tourism Department**: Used for official fee structures and accessibility guidelines.
- **OpenStreetMap**: Used to determine proximity to regional hubs and transport routes.
