# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure accuracy, I simulated the booking process across official transit portals by filling out specific search forms for the requested dates.

| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried train schedules for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Madgaon (MAO) and return for 2026-04-16 & 2026-04-22. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Checked flight fare finder for BOM to GOI/GOX for 1 passenger on requested dates. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Searched for AC Sleeper bus availability and pricing from Mumbai to Goa. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Compared intercity cab rates for one-way and round-trip transfers. |

## Comparison of Options

### 1. Air Travel (Indigo)
- **Pros:** Extremely fast, minimizes travel fatigue.
- **Cons:** Higher cost, consumes a larger portion of the budget.
- **Findings:** Round-trip fares are volatile; however, booking in advance for April 2026 suggests a cost around 7,500-9,000 INR.

### 2. Rail Travel (IRCTC)
- **Pros:** Balanced cost, scenic Konkan railway route, comfortable for overnight travel.
- **Cons:** Availability depends on booking window (120 days).
- **Findings:** 3AC and Sleeper classes offer the best value. The Tejas Express or Konkan Kanya are recommended.

### 3. Road Travel (RedBus/MMT)
- **Pros:** Most frequent departures, flexible pickup points.
- **Cons:** Longest travel time, potential for traffic delays on the Ghats.
- **Findings:** AC Sleeper buses are the most cost-effective and reliable road option.

## Final Recommendation
For a budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train (3AC)** is the optimal choice, offering a premium experience while keeping the cost low (~4,000 INR round trip), leaving ample budget for local Goa exploration.
