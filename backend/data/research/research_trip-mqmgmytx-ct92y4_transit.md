## Comparison of Transport Options

### 1. Air Travel (Fastest)
- **Pros:** Extremely short travel time, minimizes fatigue.
- **Cons:** Highest cost, consumes ~60-70% of the total trip budget.

### 2. Rail Travel (Balanced)
- **Pros:** Scenic route through the Konkan coast, very budget-friendly.
- **Cons:** Longer duration, requires early booking for 3AC berths.

### 3. Road Travel (Flexible)
- **Pros:** Direct drop-off at various Goa locations, flexible timings.
- **Cons:** Can be tiring; road conditions vary.

## Final Recommendation
For a budget of 10,000 INR, the **Train (3AC)** is the most recommended option as it provides a balance of comfort and significant cost savings for on-ground spending in Goa.
