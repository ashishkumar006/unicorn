# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Total Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure high-fidelity pricing and scheduling, simulations were performed on official booking portals. Since 2026 dates are beyond the standard 120-day booking window for Indian Railways and airlines, pricing is based on historical seasonal averages for April (Peak Summer/Spring) and current fare structures.

### Sources & Actions
| Source | Action Performed |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal** | Queried rail schedules search form for Mumbai (CSMT/LTT) to Goa (MAO/MADGAON) for April dates. |
| **Indigo Airlines Booking** | Simulated flight fare finder for 1 passenger from BOM to GOI/GOX. |
| **RedBus Portal** | Checked intercity bus search form for sleeper/semi-sleeper coach options between Mumbai and Goa. |
| **MakeMyTrip Portal** | Searched for one-way and round-trip private cab estimates for the same route. |

## Comparison Analysis

| Feature | Flight | Train | Road (Bus) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost (Round Trip)** | ~₹7,000 - ₹9,000 | ~₹2,500 - ₹4,500 | ~₹3,000 - ₹5,000 |
| **Travel Time** | 1h 15m | 12h - 15h | 14h - 16h |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium (3AC/2AC) | Medium (Sleeper) |
| **Budget Fit** | Tight | Excellent | Excellent |

## Findings
- **Flights** are the fastest but consume nearly the entire budget. 
- **Trains** (specifically the Konkan Railway route) offer the most scenic experience and are highly cost-effective.
- **Road travel** via sleeper buses provides the best flexibility in timing and drop-off points within Goa.
