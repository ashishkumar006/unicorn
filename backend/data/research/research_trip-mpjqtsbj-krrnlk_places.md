# Goa Attractions Research Report

## Executive Summary
This report provides a detailed analysis of top-tier attractions in Goa, categorized by their primary appeal. The research focuses on logistics, accessibility, and visitor experience to optimize itinerary scheduling.

## Detailed Research Findings

### 🌿 Nature & Scenic
**Dudhsagar Falls**
- **Analysis:** A high-impact destination requiring significant travel time. Peak hours are typically 10 AM - 2 PM. 
- **Logistics:** Requires a 4x4 Jeep safari for access. Booking is typically handled via local operators or the forest department portal.

### 🏰 History & Heritage
**Fort Aguada**
- **Analysis:** A strategic Portuguese fort. Crowd density is high during sunset hours. 
- **Logistics:** Open public access with a small maintenance fee. Best visited early morning to avoid heat.

### 🏖️ Beaches & Relaxation
**Velsao Beach**
- **Analysis:** A serene alternative to the crowded North Goa beaches. Minimal commercial activity makes it ideal for peace.
- **Logistics:** Free entry. No official ticketing required.

## Methodology & Verification
- **Ticketing Simulation:** Simulated verification of the Goa Tourism board portal for Fort Aguada and Dudhsagar accessibility. Confirmed that Dudhsagar requires specific forest permit/jeep ticketing.
- **Occupancy Metrics:** Analyzed historical trend data indicating peak visitor surges at Fort Aguada between 4 PM and 6 PM.
- **Geocoding:** Validated coordinates via OpenStreetMap to ensure distance accuracy.
