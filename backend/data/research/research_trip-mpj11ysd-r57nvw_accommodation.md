# Accommodation Research Report: Goa Trip (April 2026)

## 📋 Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1 Adult
- **Duration:** 7 Days (2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22)
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR total
- **Style:** Semi-Luxury

| Hotel | Base Rate/Night | Estimated 6-Night Total | Budget Status | Recommendation |

| **Shelsta Holiday Resort** | 500 INR | 3,000 INR | ✅ Well Under | Best for budget conservation. |

### 🔍 Detailed Findings

#### 1. Shelsta Holiday Resort
- **Verdict:** Highly economical. While it meets the 'semi' luxury criteria in terms of rating, the price point is significantly below the budget threshold, leaving ample room for food and activities.
- **Analysis:** Simulated booking for 1 guest confirmed availability for the requested dates. The property offers a relaxed atmosphere suitable for a solo traveler.

#### 2. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa (Marriott Series)
- **Verdict:** The 'Sweet Spot'. This property aligns perfectly with the "Semi-Luxury" request. It provides a professional hotel experience with a reputable brand backing (Marriott) while staying under the 10,000 INR cap.
- **Analysis:** Verified occupancy rules for a single guest. The rate is stable, and the spa facilities add the desired luxury touch.

#### 3. Amadi Beach Front Resort
- **Verdict:** Budget Buster. At 2,100 INR per night, the stay alone would cost 12,600 INR, which is already 26% over the *entire* trip budget of 10,000 INR.
- **Analysis:** Despite the high rating and beach-front appeal, it is financially non-viable for this specific constraint.

## 🛠 Research Methodology

### Sources Checked
- Google Places API (for initial rating and coordinates)
- Official Hotel Portals (Shelsta, Marriott, Amadi)

### Actions Performed
- **Form Simulation:** Populated `check-in date` (2026-04-16), `check-out date` (2026-04-22), `number of guests` (1), and `room type` (Standard/Single) on official booking engines.
- **Occupancy Validation:** Verified that single occupancy is permitted for the selected dates without mandatory double-occupancy surcharges.
- **Rate Verification:** Compared displayed public rates against the user's total budget limit.
