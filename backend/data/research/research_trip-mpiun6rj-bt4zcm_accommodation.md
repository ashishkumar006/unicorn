## 🔍 Deep Dive Analysis

### 💎 The Premium Choice: The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
*Best for those seeking a true semi-luxury experience with a focus on wellness and service.*
- **Highlights:** Affiliation with Marriott ensures high service standards. Includes spa facilities and high-end amenities.
- **Budget Impact:** At ₹2,100/night, a 4-night stay would cost ₹8,400, leaving ₹1,600 for incidentals.
- **Verdict:** Recommended for the "Semi-Luxury" requirement.

### 🏖️ The Balanced Choice: Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim
*Best for travelers who want to be in the heart of the action (Candolim beach/markets).*
- **Highlights:** Consistent brand quality, central location, and modern rooms.
- **Budget Impact:** Very sustainable; allows for a longer stay or higher spend on local excursions.
- **Verdict:** A reliable, high-standard mid-range option.

### 💰 The Value Choice: Shelsta Holiday Resort
*Best for maximizing the travel duration or spending the budget on luxury experiences outside the hotel.*
- **Highlights:** Surprisingly high rating for an extremely low price point.
- **Budget Impact:** Negligible impact on the 10,000 INR budget.
- **Verdict:** Great for a budget-conscious traveler who still wants a 4-star rated environment.
