## Detailed Findings

### 1. Air Travel (Indigo)
The fastest option. While flights are the most expensive, early booking for April dates typically keeps fares within the 10k budget. Indigo operates frequent shuttles from BOM to GOX (Mopa) or GOI.

### 2. Rail Travel (IRCTC)
The most cost-effective and scenic route. The Konkan Railway provides an immersive experience. 3AC is recommended for balance between cost and comfort.

### 3. Road Travel (Bus/Cab)
Private sleeper buses (Volvo/Scania) are the primary road option. Cabs are available but would exceed the 10,000 INR budget for a round trip unless shared.
