# Goa Tourism Comprehensive Guide

## 🏖️ Beaches & Coastal Gems
- **Calangute & Baga**: The heart of North Goa's party scene and water sports.
- **Palolem**: A crescent-shaped paradise in the South, ideal for relaxation and kayaking.
- **Anjuna**: Famous for its rocky shores and bohemian vibe.

## 🏛️ History & Architecture
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus**: A UNESCO World Heritage site holding the remains of St. Francis Xavier.
- **Aguada Fort**: A 17th-century Portuguese fort with a stunning lighthouse view.
- **Old Goa (Velha Goa)**: The former capital of Portuguese India, rich in colonial churches.

## 🌿 Nature & Adventure
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A majestic four-tiered waterfall cascading through the jungle.
- **Spice Plantations**: Immersive tours of vanilla, cardamom, and pepper groves.

## 💡 Travel Tips
- **Transport**: Renting a scooter or 'pilot' (motorcycle taxi) is the most efficient way to commute.
- **Season**: Visit between November and February for the best weather.
- **Dress Code**: Modest clothing is required when entering churches in Old Goa.
