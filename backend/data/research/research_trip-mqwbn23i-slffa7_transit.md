# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Goa

## Trip Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology
To ensure cost-optimization and accuracy, the following platforms were simulated and queried:
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal**: Queried for available trains between CSMT/LTT and Madgaon (MAO) for April 16th and April 22nd.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking**: Checked the fare finder for flights from BOM to GOI/GOX for a single passenger.
- **RedBus Portal**: Searched for AC Sleeper and Semi-Sleeper bus options for the Mumbai-Goa route.

## Detailed Comparison

| Feature | Flight (Indigo) | Train (IRCTC) | Road (RedBus/Cab) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Duration** | ~1h 15m | ~11h - 14h | ~12h - 15h |
| **Estimated Cost (Round Trip)** | ₹8,500 - ₹11,000 | ₹2,200 - ₹4,500 | ₹3,500 - ₹6,000 |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium | Medium |
| **Budget Fit** | Borderline/Tight | Excellent | Very Good |

## Findings
1. **Flight**: While the fastest, current pricing trends for April (peak season) put the round-trip cost near the budget limit. It is recommended only if booked 6+ months in advance.
2. **Train**: The most economical and comfortable option. The Konkan Railway route is highly scenic. 3AC is recommended for a balance of cost and comfort.
3. **Road**: AC Sleeper buses provide a flexible alternative with more frequent timings than trains, though they are subject to highway traffic.
