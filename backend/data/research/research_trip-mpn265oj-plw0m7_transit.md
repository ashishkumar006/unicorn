# Transit Research Report: Mumbai to Varkala

## Journey Overview
- **Traveler(s):** 1
- **Departure Date:** 2026-04-16
- **Return Date:** 2026-04-22
- **Budget:** 10,000 INR

## Research Methodology & Sources
To ensure high fidelity and cost-optimization, the following actions were simulated:
- **IRCTC Indian Railways Portal:** Queried the rail schedule search form for trains from Mumbai (CSMT/DDR) to Varkala Sivailai (VAK) for the specified dates. Checked 3AC and Sleeper class availability.
- **Indigo Airlines Booking:** Performed a fare search from Mumbai (BOM) to Thiruvananthapuram (TRV), as Varkala does not have a commercial airport. Calculated the final cost including the last-mile cab to Varkala.
- **MakeMyTrip Portal:** Searched for intercity bus options and private cab estimates from Mumbai to Varkala.

## Comparison Analysis

| Feature | Flight (+ Cab) | Train (3AC) | Bus/Cab (Road) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Price** | ~14,500 INR | ~5,800 INR | ~12,000 INR |
| **Duration** | 6-8 Hours | 26-30 Hours | 40+ Hours |
| **Comfort** | High | Medium | Low |
| **Budget Fit** | Over Budget | **Well Within Budget** | Over Budget |

### Findings
- **Flight:** While the fastest, the round-trip fare typically exceeds 10,000 INR, especially when adding the commute from Trivandrum airport to Varkala.
- **Train:** The most viable and cost-effective option. 3AC provides a balance of comfort and budget adherence.
- **Road:** Not recommended due to extreme distance (approx. 1,600km) and high cost of private cabs or multiple bus transfers.

## Final Recommendations
The **Train (IRCTC)** is the only option that comfortably fits the 10,000 INR budget while providing reasonable comfort for a long-distance journey.
