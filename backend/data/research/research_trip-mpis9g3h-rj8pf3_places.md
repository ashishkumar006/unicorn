# Goa Attractions Research Report

## 🏖️ Beaches
- **Calangute & Baga**: The hub of nightlife and water sports.
- **Palolem**: A serene crescent beach ideal for relaxation.

## 🏰 History & Architecture
- **Basilica of Bom Jesus**: A UNESCO World Heritage site housing the remains of St. Francis Xavier.
- **Aguada Fort**: 17th-century Portuguese fort with stunning ocean views.

## 🌿 Nature & Culture
- **Dudhsagar Falls**: A massive four-tiered waterfall in the Bhagwan Mahaveer Sanctuary.
- **Spice Plantations**: Immersive walks through aromatic spice farms.

### 💡 Travel Tips
- **Transport**: Renting a scooter or 'Pilot' (motorcycle taxi) is the most efficient way to commute.
- **Best Time to Visit**: November to February for pleasant weather.
- **Dress Code**: Modest attire is required for visiting churches and temples.
