## Regional Culinary Guide

### Must-Try Goan Specialties
To maximize the culinary experience, the traveler should look for:
1. **Fish Curry Rice:** The staple of every Goan household.
2. **Bebinca:** A multi-layered coconut milk cake.
3. **Xacuti:** A complex, spicy curry made with roasted coconut.
