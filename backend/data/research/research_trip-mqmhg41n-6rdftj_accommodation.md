# Accommodation Research Report: Goa

## Executive Summary
Research was conducted for a 7-day stay (2026-04-16 to 2026-04-22) for one traveler with a total trip budget of 10,000 INR. Given the budget constraints, the priority was to find "semi-luxury" options that balance quality with affordability. 

**Critical Budget Analysis:** The total budget of 10,000 INR for 7 days allows for a maximum spend of ~1,428 INR per night (including taxes), assuming zero spend on food or activities. Only two of the three evaluated properties fall within this strict financial limit.

### Property Insights

#### 1. Avataara Resort & Spa
- **Analysis:** This property offers the highest luxury tier (5 stars) at an exceptionally low price point. 
- **Pros:** High rating, luxury amenities, extreme value for money.
- **Cons:** Price seems too low for a 5-star resort; requires verification upon final booking.

#### 2. FabHotel Grand Royal Palms
- **Analysis:** A standard mid-range luxury option. 
- **Pros:** Budget-friendly, consistent FabHotel branding.
- **Cons:** Lower guest rating (3.7), lacks the "wow" factor of a luxury stay.

#### 3. The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa
- **Analysis:** A high-quality Marriott-affiliated property.
- **Pros:** Premium service, professional management.
- **Cons:** At ₹2,100/night, the 7-day stay (₹14,700) exceeds the entire trip budget of ₹10,000.
