### 🔍 Detailed Property Analysis

#### 🌿 The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa (Marriott Series)
*The premier choice for a "Semi-Luxury" experience.*
- **Highlights:** High brand standards, spa facilities, and a refined atmosphere.
- **Budget Impact:** At ₹2,100/night, it fits comfortably within the 10k budget for a multi-day stay while delivering a premium feel.
- **Verdict:** **Top Recommendation** for those prioritizing luxury and service.
- [Visit Official Website](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/goifk-the-fern-kadamba-hotel-and-spa-goa-panjim-series/overview/?scid=f2ae0541-1279-4f24-b197-a979c79310b0)

#### 🍋 Lemon Tree Hotel Candolim
*Perfect for accessibility and proximity to the beach.*
- **Highlights:** Known for consistent service and a cheerful, modern aesthetic. Located in the heart of Candolim.
- **Budget Impact:** Mid-range pricing allows for significant spending on dining and activities.
- **Verdict:** Best for the active traveler who wants to be near the action.
- [Visit Official Website](https://www.lemontreehotels.com/lemon-tree-hotel/goa/candolim-goa?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gmb_listing)

#### ☀️ Shelsta Holiday Resort
*The ultra-budget alternative.*
- **Highlights:** Surprisingly high rating for its price point. 
- **Budget Impact:** Negligible impact on the overall trip budget.
- **Verdict:** Recommended only if the traveler intends to spend the bulk of the 10k budget on luxury excursions rather than the room.
- [Visit Official Website](http://www.shelstaholidayresort.com/)

---

### 💡 Final Recommendation
For a **Semi-Luxury** experience, **The Fern Kadamba Hotel & Spa** is the superior choice. It aligns with the style request through its Marriott affiliation and spa offerings, while remaining well within the ₹10,000 total budget.
